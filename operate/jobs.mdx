**Jobs** lists your last 100 contact searches, imports, and enrichment jobs. Pipeline runs are not listed here; they appear in **Activity**.

To open it, select **Jobs** in the menu, under **Add Contacts**. **Our search** and the import pages under **Add to Pipeline** also link to it.

As a member, you see the jobs of your own seats. As an admin, you see the whole account.

## Read the jobs table

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Started** | When the job started |
| **Type** | The kind of job, such as **Contact search**, **Automatic search**, **CSV import**, or **Contact enrichment** |
| **Rep** | The seat the job ran for |
| **Status** | Whether the job is still running, succeeded, or failed, with the reason in plain words when something went wrong |
| **Scope** | What the job covered: companies searched, URLs submitted, or contacts |
| **Results** | For a search: leads returned → leads kept and added to your contacts |
| **Credits** | Credits charged for the job |
| **Duration** | How long the job took |
| **CSV** | Download for a finished search |

A search can return leads that fail your ideal customer profile (ICP) filters, so the kept number is often lower than the returned number. Credits pay for returned results, not only for the leads kept.

## Download a search as CSV

Select **CSV** on a finished search to download every lead it returned, kept and rejected. Each row says whether the lead was kept and, if not, why it was rejected. Use it to check how well your targeting matches what the search finds.

## When a search stops early

If credits run out during a search, the job still finishes and its status shows how many companies were **not searched: not enough credits**. Admins can follow the link to **Credits** to top up; members ask an admin.

When a member's search finds leads that belong to a colleague's territory, those leads go to the colleague's seat and the row says how many went there.

Select **Refresh** to update the list while a job is running.

## When a job fails

A failed job shows what you can do about it, in the same words as on **Activity**:

- **LinkedIn session expired — reconnect it under Settings → Account.**
- **Email account disconnected — reconnect it under Settings → Account.**
- **Another search was already running — start it again once that one is done.**
- **Credits could not be charged — check your balance under Credits.**
- **The blacklist could not be read, so nothing was searched — try again.**

Any other error reads **Something went wrong — we've been notified.**
