Open **Messages** to review generated outreach. What appears there depends on the delivery mode under **Settings → Schedule**.

## Message actions

### Edit

Change the subject or body while keeping the existing research and strategy. Use this for a targeted correction.

### Regenerate

Discard the current draft and create a new message. Use regeneration when the approach is wrong, not for a small wording change.

### Reject

Remove the message from the send queue. Rejecting the message does not delete the contact or company.

### Approve

Approve a LinkedIn message so it becomes eligible for outreach. Approval does not send immediately; the message waits for the next outreach run and the seat's daily limit.

## Review the strategy, not only grammar

Expand a message to check its strategy brief and confirm:

- the person and company are correct
- the message reflects the current role and context
- the proof point is accurate and permitted
- the call to action matches the persona
- the language and formality are appropriate
- the sign-off matches the representative
- the message makes no unsupported claim

<Warning>
  AI-generated text can be factually wrong or inappropriate even when it reads naturally. Automatic delivery transfers review responsibility to you; it does not remove it.
</Warning>

## Email draft mode

When email delivery is set to drafts, emails are created in the connected mailbox. Review and send them from Gmail or Outlook. LinkedIn approvals remain in the Sales Agent dashboard.

## After changing the playbook

Existing drafts do not automatically become new drafts. Regenerate affected messages or reject them and let the agent prepare replacements. Keep approval enabled until the updated output is stable.

See [Messaging playbook](/configure/messaging-playbook) for the settings that shape new messages.
