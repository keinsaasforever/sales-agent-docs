Stages and outcomes answer different questions:

- A **stage** says what has happened so far. The Sales Agent derives it from recorded activity.
- An **outcome** records a human decision or sales result. Every outcome stops automated outreach.

## Stages are automatic

You cannot manually select **Invited**, **Connected**, **Messaged**, or **Replied**. Those stages appear when the corresponding action or event is recorded.

Examples:

- sending a connection request produces **Invite Sent**
- detecting an accepted request produces **Connected**
- sending an initial email or LinkedIn message produces **Messaged**
- detecting a reply produces **Replied**

See the [pipeline stage reference](/reference/pipeline-stages) for every stage.

## Outcomes are manual controls

Open a prospect and use the outcome selector:

| Outcome | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **Meeting** | A meeting is booked or the conversation has entered your direct sales process. |
| **Offer Sent** | You sent a commercial proposal. |
| **Closed Deal** | The opportunity was won. |
| **Not Interested** | The prospect declined and should receive no more automated outreach. |
| **Lost Deal** | A progressed opportunity was lost. |
| **Unreachable** | The available automated channels are exhausted or invalid. |

Every option displays “stops outreach” because it removes the prospect from all automated steps.

## Use In play carefully

**In play** means no outcome is set. Returning a stopped prospect to **In play** can make it eligible again based on its existing history.

<Warning>
  Changing an outcome back to **In play** does not erase sent messages, follow-up counts, replies, or connection history. The next eligible action is derived from those existing facts.
</Warning>

## Archive versus outcome

Archiving hides a prospect and stops automation without changing its outcome. Unarchiving restores the previous state. If the prospect still has an outcome, it remains stopped after unarchiving.

Use an outcome to record why the sales process ended or moved to a person. Use archive to remove a record from the active view without rewriting its history.
