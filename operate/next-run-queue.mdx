The **Home → Next run** cards predict the work the next complete pipeline run can perform. They use the same eligibility rules and daily limits as the pipeline.

## Read a queue card

Each card shows:

- **ready count** — contacts eligible for that step now
- **daily limit** — maximum work the active seats can perform in that step
- **run count** — the smaller of ready count and limit
- **queued remainder** — eligible contacts that stay queued because of the cap
- **preview** — examples of contacts at the front of the queue

For example, `10 / 20` means 10 contacts are ready and the limit allows up to 20, so all 10 can run. If 35 contacts are ready with a limit of 20, the card shows 20 and leaves 15 queued.

## The five queue cards

| Card | Ready when |
| --- | --- |
| **Connection requests** | The contact has an owner, passes the pipeline gate, has no open stop condition, and is eligible for LinkedIn. |
| **Messages** | Research is available and no initial message has been generated yet. |
| **Outreach** | An email or LinkedIn message is approved and has not been sent. |
| **Follow-up email** | An email was sent, the next follow-up is due, the cap is not reached, and no reply or terminal thread state exists. |
| **Follow-up LinkedIn** | A LinkedIn message was sent, the follow-up is due, the cap is not reached, and neither channel has a reply. |

Research and enrichment can occur between the visible cards. They do not have a separate user-configurable queue tile.

## Why a contact may not be ready

A contact can be held because:

- its ICP score is below the pipeline threshold
- it has no active owner or usable channel
- research or enrichment is still missing
- its message is waiting for approval
- the next follow-up is not due yet
- the daily limit is `0` or already consumed
- it replied, has an outcome, or is archived
- its company is blacklisted
- the connected account needs attention

<Note>
  The queue is a forecast, not a promise that every action will succeed. Account state, source data, reply checks, or external platform responses can still cause an item to be skipped safely during the run.
</Note>
