The **Home → Next run** cards predict the work the next complete pipeline run can perform. They use the same eligibility rules and daily limits as the pipeline.

## Read a queue card

Each card shows:

| Field | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready count** | Number of contacts currently eligible for that step |
| **Daily limit** | Maximum actions your active seats can take in that step today |
| **Run count** | The smaller of ready count and daily limit — what will actually run |
| **Queued remainder** | Eligible contacts that stay queued because the cap is full |
| **Preview** | A sample of the contacts at the front of the queue |

**Example:** If 35 contacts are ready for outreach but the daily limit is 20, the card shows a run count of 20 and a queued remainder of 15. Those 15 stay in queue and are prioritised on the next run.

<Note>
  The queue is a forecast, not a guarantee that every action will succeed. Account state, source data, reply checks, or platform responses can still cause individual items to be safely skipped during the run.
</Note>

## The five queue cards

| Card | A contact is ready when… |
| --- | --- |
| **Connection requests** | The contact has an owner, passes the pipeline gate, has no open stop condition, and is eligible for LinkedIn outreach. |
| **Messages** | Research is available and no initial message has been generated for this contact yet. |
| **Outreach** | An email or LinkedIn message is approved and has not yet been sent. |
| **Follow-up email** | An email was previously sent, the next follow-up date is due, the cap is not reached, and no reply or terminal thread state exists. |
| **Follow-up LinkedIn** | A LinkedIn message was sent, the follow-up date is due, the cap is not reached, and neither channel has recorded a reply. |

Research and enrichment steps can occur between visible cards. They do not have a separate user-configurable queue tile.

## Why a contact may not be ready

If a contact is absent from the queue, one of these conditions is holding it back:

- Its ICP score falls below the pipeline threshold
- It has no active owner or no usable outreach channel
- Research or enrichment data is still missing
- Its generated message is waiting for your approval
- The next follow-up is not yet due
- The daily limit is set to `0` or has already been consumed for today
- It has replied, has an outcome set, or has been archived
- Its company name or domain is on the blacklist
- The connected LinkedIn or email account needs attention

Work through this list top-to-bottom whenever a contact you expect to see is missing. Most cases resolve by checking the contact's profile, the seat's account status, or the limit configuration.
