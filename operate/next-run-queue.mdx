The **Next run** cards on **Home** predict the work the next complete pipeline run can perform. They use the same eligibility rules and daily limits as the pipeline.

As a member, the cards cover your own seats. As an admin, they cover every active seat in your account.

## Read a queue card

Each card shows:

| Part | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Number** | How many contacts the next run will work in this step: the contacts that are ready, capped by the daily limit |
| **Footer** | **N more queued (capped)** when more contacts are ready than the limit allows, **All ready will run**, or **No contacts ready** |
| **Names** | Up to five contacts at the front of the queue, with their company |
| **Tooltip** | Hover the card to see what it counts and its daily limit |

**Example:** If 35 contacts are ready for outreach but the daily limit is 20, the card shows 20 and **15 more queued (capped)**. Those 15 stay in the queue for a later run.

<Note>
  The queue is a forecast, not a guarantee that every action will succeed. Account state, source data, reply checks, or platform responses can still cause individual items to be safely skipped during the run. Limits count per day: if a run already went out today, a second run on the same day only uses what is left of each limit.
</Note>

## The five queue cards

| Card | A contact is ready when… |
| --- | --- |
| **New Prospects** | The contact has an owner, meets the minimum ICP score, has not entered the pipeline yet, and its company has no deal stage. With LinkedIn on, the card also counts prospects waiting for their LinkedIn invite. |
| **Message** | Research is complete and the prospect still needs a first message on a channel its seat can send. |
| **Outreach** | An email or LinkedIn message is approved, not sent yet, and not held back. A LinkedIn message also needs its invite to have gone out. |
| **Follow-up Email** | An email was sent, the next follow-up is due, the follow-up cap is not reached, and there is no reply or closed thread. |
| **Follow-up LinkedIn** | A LinkedIn message was sent, the follow-up is due, the cap is not reached, and neither channel has recorded a reply. |

**New Prospects** takes on fewer new people when contacts are already waiting for an invite or a first email, so the backlog cannot grow faster than your seats can send. When LinkedIn is off or paused on every seat, **New Prospects** counts only new contacts and **Follow-up LinkedIn** stays at 0.

Research and enrichment steps can occur between visible cards. They do not have a separate user-configurable queue tile.

## Why a contact may not be ready

If a contact is absent from the queue, one of these conditions is holding it back:

- Its ICP score falls below the pipeline threshold
- It has no active owner or no usable outreach channel
- Its company already has a stage, such as **Meeting** or **Not interesting**, so colleagues are not taken on
- It is parked because it may no longer work at the company
- Research or enrichment data is still missing
- Its generated message is waiting for your approval, or is held back (see [Review messages](/operate/review-messages))
- The next follow-up is not yet due, or an out-of-office reply moved it to the return date
- The daily limit is set to `0` or has already been consumed for today
- It has replied, has an outcome set, or has been archived
- Its company name or domain is on the blacklist
- The connected LinkedIn or email account needs attention, including a mailbox your provider blocked from sending (see [Activity and troubleshooting](/operate/activity-and-troubleshooting#if-your-mail-provider-blocks-sending))

Work through this list top-to-bottom whenever a contact you expect to see is missing. Most cases resolve by checking the contact's profile, the seat's account status, or the limit configuration.
