The Sales Agent monitors replies so automated outreach does not continue after a prospect engages.

## Cross-channel stop behavior

A reply detected on email stops LinkedIn follow-ups. A reply detected on LinkedIn stops email follow-ups. This prevents the other channel from continuing a sequence after the person has responded.

The prospect moves to **Replied** unless a later human outcome such as **Meeting** or **Closed Deal** is already set.

## Take over the conversation

When a reply arrives:

1. read the complete thread in the source channel
2. respond personally from the connected LinkedIn or email account
3. record useful context or notes in the prospect
4. set **Meeting** when the conversation enters your sales process
5. advance the outcome to **Offer Sent**, **Closed Deal**, or **Lost Deal** as appropriate

Your direct reply and intervention should not compete with automation. The reply flag or selected outcome keeps the prospect out of automated claims.

## To Call

The **To Call** stage appears when the configured automated sequence is complete and a usable phone number exists. It can also appear when digital channels are exhausted but phone outreach remains possible.

After the call, set the outcome that reflects the result. Leaving the prospect **In play** can allow another eligible automated action if its existing sequence is not fully complete.

## Out-of-office and delivery problems

An out-of-office email can defer the next follow-up until the return date. A failed email delivery can trigger another contact-data check when an alternative is available. These cases do not count as a positive reply.

<Tip>
  Handle reply notifications before reviewing new outreach. The fastest path to value is taking over active conversations, not filling the next queue first.
</Tip>
