The Sales Agent monitors both email and LinkedIn for prospect replies and stops automated follow-ups the moment engagement is detected. Knowing exactly when and how handoff happens lets you take over at the right moment without competing with automation.

## Cross-channel stop behavior

A reply on **either** channel stops follow-ups on **both** channels:

- A reply detected on **email** stops pending LinkedIn follow-ups for that prospect.
- A reply detected on **LinkedIn** stops pending email follow-ups for that prospect.

This prevents the other channel from continuing its sequence after the person has already responded. The cross-channel stop is deliberate. Even if your LinkedIn message sparked a reply that only arrived in email, the Sales Agent treats the engagement as complete contact and halts the automated sequence on both sides.

## How replies are sorted

The Sales Agent reads each reply and sorts it. The **Reply** column on **Prospects** shows the result.

| Reply | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Interested, or not judged yet | Stage **Replied** |
| A LinkedIn pleasantry such as "thanks for connecting" | Stage **Replied**, shown as **Neutral**. The next message is judged again. |
| A no | Stage **Declined**, no outcome |
| A later time, such as "next quarter" | Stage **Not right now**, no outcome |
| A request to stop | Outcome **Asked to stop** |
| Not the right person | Stage **Declined**. If the reply names someone else, the **Reply** column shows **Referral** and the timeline shows **Referred to** with the name. An automatic reply saying the person has left the company also sets them aside as **Changed employer**; see [Contacts held back](/operate/prospects-and-companies#contacts-held-back). |

**Declined** and **Not right now** keep the prospect in play, so you can approach them again later. **Asked to stop** is an outcome and keeps them out of all automated outreach.

## Check the verdict

Open a prospect who replied. The **Info** tab starts with a **They replied** card that shows the reply and its date, with the automatic verdict already selected. Select **Positive**, **Not right now**, or **Negative** to correct it, or select the chosen one again to clear it. A reply that ended as **Asked to stop**, **Wrong person**, or **Email bounced** is shown but cannot be changed here: change the outcome instead. **Later** hides the card until you open the prospect again.

Marking a reply **Positive** puts the company **In conversation** and stops outreach to the prospect's colleagues. See [the deal lives on the company](/operate/stages-and-outcomes#the-deal-lives-on-the-company).

## Take over the conversation

When a reply arrives, move quickly and personally:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the thread">
    Select **Reply now** on the reply card to open the exact email or LinkedIn thread in a new tab. The **Timeline** tab has **View Email** and **View LinkedIn**, each naming the mailbox or seat it belongs to. On the **Prospects** list, hover the **Email Sent** or **LinkedIn Sent** date or the **Reply** badge for the same buttons. A thread opens only if you are signed in to that mailbox or that seat's LinkedIn. When the exact thread is not known, the button is hidden.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond personally">
    Reply directly from your connected LinkedIn or email account. Your personal reply is the handoff. Do not use the Sales Agent to send further automated messages once a conversation is live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add context to the prospect record">
    On the **Notes** tab, use **Add note** to record next steps or background. Each note shows who wrote it, and only the author can edit or delete it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Meeting outcome">
    As soon as the conversation enters your sales process, set the outcome to **Meeting**. This confirms the handoff and ensures automation does not reclaim the prospect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Advance the outcome as the deal progresses">
    Update to **Offer Sent**, **Closed Deal**, **Not interesting**, or **Lost Deal** as the opportunity moves forward. Keeping the outcome current gives you accurate pipeline visibility.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Home and **Tasks** show how many prospects replied and are waiting on you. A prospect leaves that count when you set an outcome or mark the reply **Negative** or **Not right now**.

## Call the To Call list

The **To Call** tab on **Prospects** lists prospects with a working phone number who are due a call. By default, a prospect joins once all follow-ups are done. Admins can change this in **Settings → Targeting** under **Call list** (**Show a lead on the To Call tab**): **Never**, **When the invite is sent**, **When the invite is accepted**, **When the first message goes out**, or **When all follow-ups are done (default)**. The setting never changes what the agent sends or when.

A prospect marked **Unreachable** who has a number is always on the list. A prospect who replied is not.

**To Call** is a signal, not an automated action. It means the next step belongs to you.

### Log a call

The **Call** card on the prospect's **Info** tab shows the number to dial. Select **Yes, I called**, add an optional summary, and choose the result:

| Result | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Positive** | Outcome **Meeting**. Stops outreach. |
| **Negative** | Outcome **Asked to stop**. Stops outreach. |
| **Wrong person** | Outcome **Wrong person**. Stops outreach. |
| **Nobody answered** | Logged with your note. Outreach goes on and the prospect stays on **To Call**. |

If the prospect already has **Offer Sent**, **Closed Deal**, or **Lost Deal**, a logged call keeps that outcome. The card counts unanswered tries. **Not yet** hides the card for now. A recorded call shows who logged it, and **Clear outcome** removes the result and restores the earlier outcome.

### When the number is wrong

Under the number, select **Number doesn't work** or **Wrong person on this number** (someone else answers). These mark the number, not the person: outreach continues and the next number on file takes over. In the **Enrichment** section, every number has **Works**, **Doesn't work**, and **Wrong person** buttons, and older numbers are behind **more numbers**. When you mark the last number as not working, the Sales Agent asks whether to look for a new one, which may use credits.

If the lookup found nothing, the section says **No phone found**. Select **Add number** to enter a number you already know, or **Find phone** to start a lookup.

## Out-of-office and delivery problems

Not all replies indicate real engagement:

- An **out-of-office** reply is not a reply. The Sales Agent reads the return date and holds both email and LinkedIn follow-ups until the working day after it, for at most 90 days. Without a readable date, it waits five working days. The timeline's **Current state** shows **Paused — out of office** with the first possible follow-up date, and the **Reply** column shows **Out of office**. A real reply or a bounce ends the pause.
- An auto-reply saying the person has **left the company** is treated as not the right person, not as an absence.
- When an auto-reply gives the person's **new email address**, follow-ups go to that address.
- A **bounced email** marks that address as not working, and it is never used again. The Sales Agent looks for another address, which may use credits. If it finds one, the sequence continues there. If not, the prospect continues on LinkedIn only, or becomes **Unreachable** when LinkedIn is not available.

These cases are handled automatically. You do not need to intervene unless the prospect remains stuck after the expected return date or after the address lookup completes.

<Tip>
  Handle replies before reviewing new outreach. Taking over an active conversation delivers more value than filling the next queue. Set your outcomes before running the next pipeline step so you don't reach contacts who have already responded.
</Tip>
