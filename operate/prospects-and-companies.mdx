**Prospects** holds every person you know, in two tabs: **Contacts** lists everyone added or found, and **Prospects** lists the people the outreach pipeline works. **Companies** shows the same people grouped by company. To add people or companies yourself, open **Add Prospects** or **Add Companies**; see [Add people or companies by hand](/configure/add-prospects#add-people-or-companies-by-hand).

## Contacts and prospects

On the **Contacts** tab, the **Stage** column shows the stage of each contact who is already a prospect. A contact who is not in outreach yet shows **Queued** when the first pipeline step will pick them up, or **Not in pipeline** when it will not. Hover the badge to see why. The **Company match** column shows **Needs review**, **Confirmed wrong**, or **Changed employer** for [contacts held back](#contacts-held-back). The **ICP** column shows the contact's score as a chip colored by its band; see [Read the score chips](/configure/icp-and-scoring#read-the-score-chips).

**Phone** and **Email** sit after **Job Title** on both tabs. **Email** is the address the Sales Agent would send to. **Phone** is the number to dial: select it to call, or read **Not working** for a number that was tried and does not work, and **Not found** once a lookup came back empty. For a person who asked to stop, or whose company is on your blacklist, the number and the address show greyed instead of as links; hover one to see why. **Not interesting** is your own verdict, so those links stay.

The **Intent** column shows the buying intent of the person's company, from 0 to 100: how many fresh, strong [buying signals](/configure/icp-and-scoring#set-buying-signals) it shows, counting the signals their persona hears about. From 60, the number carries a flame. The column appears on **Contacts** and **Prospects** only once someone has intent. On **Companies**, the column is called **Buying intent**.

Chips next to the view picker filter a list in one click, and select a pressed chip again to clear it:

- **Buying signals · N** keeps the rows with buying intent, on **Companies**, **Contacts**, and **Prospects**.
- **Needs review · N** keeps the companies that need a decision, on **Companies**.
- **Email to check · N** keeps the contacts with an address waiting for a check, on **Contacts**.

A chip shows only while it has rows, and its number is what a click shows. On **Prospects**, it counts within the open stage tab.

The **Prospects** tab has one tab per stage (see [Stages and outcomes](/operate/stages-and-outcomes)). Select a row to open the prospect. On the list, select the **Email Sent** or **LinkedIn Sent** date or the **Reply** badge to open the prospect's timeline directly, or hover them to open the email or LinkedIn thread.

Select several rows to act on them together: **Set outcome…**, **Archive**, **Find email**, **Find phone**, or **Find both**. On **Prospects**, rows whose LinkedIn was skipped also offer **Retry LinkedIn**. Admins also see **Hand over**, which moves people to another seat; see [Hand people or a company to another seat](#hand-people-or-a-company-to-another-seat). Lookups may use credits.

## The prospect record

A prospect opens in a side panel with the outcome selector and **Archive** next to the name, and four tabs.

### Info

- **They replied** and **Call**: the reply and the call cards, when they apply. See [Replies and handoff](/operate/replies-and-handoff).
- **Why now**: appears when signals were found for the person's company. See [Why now](#why-now).
- **Contact**: select a field to edit it. **Language**, **Country**, and **Seniority** are chosen from a list, so a value is always stored the same way. **Language** offers the languages the contact's seat writes in, and **Country** is the person's own, not the company's. **Persona** decides which message prompt the contact is written with. A **Skipped** row means LinkedIn is closed for this prospect and outreach continues by email only.
- **Enrichment**: every email address and phone number found, each with its status. Select **Bad** on an email address that is wrong: the Sales Agent marks it and looks for another one. For phone numbers, see [when the number is wrong](/operate/replies-and-handoff#when-the-number-is-wrong). **Find email**, **Find phone**, and **Find both** appear when something is missing.
- **Company**: the company's details, editable, and **Open company page**. The size **Range** is chosen from LinkedIn's headcount ranges.
- **Cheat sheet**: prep notes for a call. Select **Edit** to change them.

### Timeline

The top of the tab has **View Email** and **View LinkedIn** to open the thread. **Current state** then says why outreach is stopped or paused, for example **Outreach ended · Meeting** with the reason you gave, **Paused — out of office**, or **LinkedIn stopped** with the reason. Below it, every event is listed with its date: connection requests, messages drafted, approved, and sent, replies, notes, and calls. Select **Show more** to read a full message. A LinkedIn message that did not go out shows **Not delivered** with the reason LinkedIn gave.

### Notes

Use **Add note** to record context. Notes are listed newest first, with the author's name, the date, and **edited** once changed. Only the author can edit or delete a note. A note without an author, such as one from an import, can be edited by anyone and deleted by no one. Logged calls appear here with their result.

### Colleagues

Other people at the same company, with their stage. Select one to open them. When nobody else is on file yet, **Find more** opens a search for people at the company. A colleague stopped by a company stage shows **Keep in outreach**. A member may see **A colleague is already in touch with this company.** when someone on another seat has contacted a person there.

## Contacts held back

The Sales Agent keeps some contacts out of outreach until you decide. Open the contact from the **Contacts** tab, or the prospect from the **Prospects** tab, to see the notice and its buttons at the top of the **Info** tab. Both panels offer the same buttons. A prospect held back shows **Parked — company mismatch** or **Parked — changed employer** under **Current state** on its timeline.

- **Needs review**: the email address belongs to another company, so the person may not work at the company you targeted. Select **This is the right company — add to outreach**, or **Confirm wrong — keep out**. A contact you confirmed shows **Confirmed wrong** and can still be added with **Actually the right company — add to outreach**.
- **Changed employer**: the person's job history, or an automatic reply from their mailbox, says they left the company. A person who asks not to be contacted in such a reply is never filed here. The notice names the new employer when it is known, and their old address is not used. If they still work there, select **Still works here — keep in outreach**.
- **Email address to check**: a search, an import, or an enrichment returned an address on a domain not confirmed as the company's, so it was not used. The person is not set aside for it. Admins can select **Use this email**, which saves the address for that company and checks it once, or **Not their address**. Members see the address and can ask an admin.

When addresses wait for a check, admins see a task on Home and in **Tasks** saying how many contacts have an email address waiting for your check. **Check** opens the **Contacts** tab filtered to **Email to check**.

## Companies

**Companies** lists every company in your pipeline. The **Stage** column shows the deal stage you set, in solid green, or otherwise the furthest stage any prospect there has reached. **Primary contact** names the person the deal is with, and **Company Fit** is the company's own 0–100 score, shown as a chip colored by its band (see [Read the score chips](/configure/icp-and-scoring#read-the-score-chips)).

Open a company to see:

- **Why now**: the company's buying intent and the signals behind it. See [Why now](#why-now).
- **Deal**: the company's stage. See [the deal lives on the company](/operate/stages-and-outcomes#the-deal-lives-on-the-company).
- **Identity** and **Firmographics**: website, LinkedIn page, location, industry, headcount, and similar details.
- **Scoring**: the company's **Company Fit** and, when available, **Why**. Hover the info icon next to **Company Fit** (**About this score**) to see what it is made of: the fit from the facts it is scored on, the AI check (for example **AI check +10**, or **No AI check yet**), and a line like **Not known: size, counts as a pass** when a filter you set could not be judged because the fact is missing. Admins can set **Company Fit** by hand; see [Set a company's fit by hand](#set-a-companys-fit-by-hand).
- **Contacts**: everyone at the company with their stage, **Make primary**, and **Keep in outreach** for colleagues stopped by the deal stage.

### Why now

When signals were found for a company, the company, contact, and prospect panels show a **Why now** section near the top; on a person it reads **Why now at** and the company's name. Next to its title, the number is the company's buying intent. It never shows for a company without signals.

Each card is one signal: its type, such as **Acquisition**, **New leader**, **Funding**, **Expansion**, or **Job ads**, its date or **no date**, a short summary, a score from 0 to 100, and a link to the source. The counting signals come first, newest first, and three show at once; select **Show all** for the rest. A signal that no longer counts is dimmed and reads **ended**, or **not counted** when its type is not one you picked. The section ends with the date the company was last checked. On a person, only the signals their persona hears about count. Set what counts under [Settings → Signals](/configure/icp-and-scoring#set-buying-signals).

### Set a company's fit by hand

Admins can replace the AI's judgment of a company. In the company panel, under **Scoring**, select the **Company Fit** number, type a whole number from 0 to 100, and press `Enter`. The number then shows **by hand** and a pencil mark. The AI check skips the company from then on, and its people blend 50/50 with your number. A number below 50 also caps their scores at that number. Hover the info icon next to the number for the date.

To give the decision back to the AI, select the number, empty the field, and press `Enter`; the company is scored again. The option is not offered where your scores come from outside the Sales Agent.

### Review duplicate companies

When the Sales Agent suspects that two companies in your list are one, the company shows a **Needs review** box that names the other one. The **Needs review · N** chip above the list filters to these companies, and admins see a task on Home. Only admins decide:

- **Same company** merges the two at once. The people, signals, and history of the newer company move to the older one, and the older one fills only the details it lacks. The newer company leaves **Companies**, search, and the **Add to Pipeline** company picker. A toast says how many people, signals, and history entries moved. The dashboard has no undo for a merge, so contact support if you merged by mistake.
- **Different companies** keeps both and ends the question.

If one of the two companies has a deal stage, it applies to everyone at the merged company. The same box also asks whether a company may be insolvent; see the [glossary](/reference/glossary).

## Export a list as CSV

Admins can download a list with **Export** in the toolbar of **Companies**, **Prospects → Contacts**, and **Prospects → Prospects**. The file holds the rows the table shows, in its order, with more columns than the table has. For prospects, that includes every message with its subject, text, status, and sent date, the stage and outreach dates, the replies, call results, and notes.

When no filter, stage tab, or toggle narrows the list, **Export** asks only for the format. Otherwise, **Export as CSV** shows how many rows the view holds and the filters as chips: **Export N** downloads the view, and **Export all N** downloads the whole list. Pick the format that matches where you open the file:

- **For Excel or a CRM import** separates columns with commas, as CRM imports and Excel in an English region expect. It is preselected.
- **For German Excel** separates columns with semicolons, which Excel opens in columns in a German region.

Members do not see **Export**.

## Hand people or a company to another seat

Admins can hand people to another seat, so that seat's LinkedIn account and mailbox reach them. Select **Hand over** in a contact, prospect, or company panel. On **Contacts** and **Prospects**, select rows and use **Hand over (N)**. The button appears only when that person would move.

1. Choose the seat. Your own seat is not in the list, because you hand people away.
2. Choose the scope: **Their whole company (recommended)** moves everyone there who has not been contacted yet, together with the company itself. **Only this person** or **Only these people** moves just them. Handing over whole companies works for up to 50 companies at a time.
3. Read the summary, which lists who moves, who stays, and the company rows that move, then confirm with **Hand to** and the seat's name.

Only people nobody has contacted yet change seat. A person with a sent message, a reply, a call, or a deal outcome stays, because that conversation lives in the old seat's LinkedIn account and mailbox. When a company is split between two seats, the summary warns you: someone who sees only one seat does not see the other seat's replies, and a meeting or positive reply on either one stops outreach to everyone at the company.

Unsent drafts of the people who move are deleted, and the new seat writes its own. New people found in a country that belongs to another seat still go to that seat; the summary names it. While a run is active on one of the seats involved, wait until it has finished.
