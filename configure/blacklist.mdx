Use **Settings → Blacklist** to prevent the Sales Agent from contacting companies you should never reach, and, under **Do not contact**, single people who asked not to be contacted. Only admins of your account see this page. The blacklist is checked during every search, import, and pipeline run.

Typical entries include:

- **Existing customers** — companies already in a commercial relationship with you
- **Active opportunities** — deals currently in your CRM pipeline
- **Partners** — referral or technology partners you should not cold-contact
- **Competitors** — organizations you do not want to tip off
- **Employers or affiliated companies** — organizations with a conflict of interest
- **Opt-outs** — companies that have explicitly asked not to be contacted

## Add entries

In the **Blocked Companies & Domains** card, enter one or both of the following and select **Add**:

- **Company name** — matched against names found across sources
- **Domain** — matched against the company's web domain (e.g., `acme.com`)

To add many entries at once, select **Upload CSV**. The file needs a company name column, a domain column, or both; a single column without a header is read as company names.

The Sales Agent normalizes names and domains and silently skips entries that already exist on the list. An entry also matches a company under the other domains and names it is known by, such as a country site.

**Prefer domains over names.** Company names vary across sources — the same business may appear as "Acme Corp," "Acme Corporation," or "ACME." A domain is a more reliable identifier and produces fewer missed matches.

Free email domains such as `gmail.com` are refused. Blocking one would stop nobody, because matching works per company. If you add one on its own, the line under the form says so: **gmail.com is a free email provider, so blocking it would not stop anyone.** If you add a company name together with a free domain, or upload a file that has some, the entry goes in without the domain, and the summary reads **Left out N free email domains**. A row left with neither a name nor a domain is skipped. To stop one person, use [**Do not contact**](#stop-outreach-to-one-person).

The result of **Add** or **Upload CSV** appears right under the form, so you see it without scrolling past the list. The list below scrolls in its own box, with the column headings in view.

Companies also land on the list automatically when you mark a **Meeting**, **Offer Sent**, or **Closed Deal** with them. See [When a company says yes](/configure/schedule-and-limits#choose-delivery-and-company-stops).

## Understand the immediate effect

Saving a blacklist entry triggers reconciliation across the entire live pipeline — not just future searches.

<Warning>
  As soon as you save an entry, any prospects in the active pipeline that match the company or domain are stopped immediately. There is no confirmation step, so review the name and domain carefully before you select **Add** or upload a file. The summary after saving shows how many prospects in your pipeline were stopped.
</Warning>

Stopped prospects are archived rather than deleted. Their full history — messages sent, replies, enrichment data — remains accessible. No outreach is sent to them after the entry is saved.

## Remove an entry

To remove a company from the blacklist, find the entry in **Settings → Blacklist** and delete it with the trash icon. Removal triggers a re-evaluation of prospects that were archived because of that block, and a message pops up with **Entry removed** and how many prospects were reactivated. A failed delete also shows as a message, so the list never shifts under your cursor.

Archived prospects can become active again if:

- no other blacklist entry still matches them, and
- no separate outcome (reply, manual stop, or archive) is already recorded for them.

## Stop outreach to one person

The **Do not contact** card, below the blacklist, holds people who asked not to be contacted again. It shows how many people are on the list. Nobody on it is contacted or comes back as a lead, even after their contact is deleted, and a person the Sales Agent marks **Asked to stop** goes on it too.

For an objection that reached you by phone or letter, enter the person's email address or LinkedIn profile URL and select **Add a person**. The line under the card says **Added**, **Added · N prospects in your pipeline stopped**, or **Already on the list**. Only a fingerprint of what you enter is kept, so the list cannot show it back to you. To remove someone's data altogether on their request, see [Delete a person everywhere](/operate/prospects-and-companies#delete-a-person-everywhere).

## Plan ahead before large imports

<Tip>
  Add all relevant entries to the blacklist before running a large CSV import or Search. Blocking a company after the agent has already researched and enriched its contacts wastes the credits spent on that work — and stops prospects mid-pipeline rather than preventing them from entering it.
</Tip>

For guidance on importing contacts, see [Add Prospects](/configure/add-prospects).
