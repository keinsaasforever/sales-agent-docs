title: "Blacklist"
description: "Exclude customers, competitors, partners, and active opportunities from outreach."

---

Use **Settings → Blacklist** to prevent outreach to companies you should not contact.

Typical entries include:

- existing customers
- active opportunities
- partners
- competitors
- employers or affiliated companies
- companies that asked not to be contacted

## Add entries

Enter a company name, a domain, or both. You can also upload a CSV. The Sales Agent normalizes names and domains and skips duplicates.

Use a domain whenever possible. Company names can vary across sources, while the domain is usually a stronger identifier.

## Understand the immediate effect

The blacklist is checked when the agent searches for contacts and when you import a CSV. Saving also reconciles the live pipeline.

<Warning>
  Saving an entry immediately stops matching prospects already in the pipeline. Review the company and domain before you save.
</Warning>

Stopped prospects are archived rather than deleted, so their history remains available. The save confirmation tells you how many live prospects were stopped.

## Remove an entry

Removing a blacklist entry re-evaluates prospects that were archived by that block. Prospects can become active again if no other blacklist entry matches and no separate outcome stops them.

<Tip>
  Add the blacklist before a large CSV import or search. This prevents avoidable research and outreach work rather than stopping it afterward.
</Tip>
