The messaging playbook tells the Sales Agent what to say and how to say it. Configure it under **Settings → Prompts**.

## Build the shared message strategy

Use the playbook to make the following explicit:

- positioning and the problem you solve
- the proof or success stories the agent may use
- useful hooks for the target audience
- words, claims, and approaches to use or avoid
- the offer you make before asking for a call
- compliance or market-specific restrictions
- different approaches for personas, champions, and decision-makers

Concrete examples teach style better than adjectives such as “professional” or “friendly.” Include one to three messages you would be comfortable sending yourself.

## Configure each seat's voice

Email and LinkedIn have separate writing-style fields because the channels behave differently. Adjust tone, greeting, length, formality, and sentence style for the representative who owns the seat.

For multilingual outreach, configure a sign-off for each supported language.

## Keep sign-offs and signatures separate

The sender assembles each email in this order:

1. generated message body
2. language-specific email sign-off
3. email signature

The LinkedIn sign-off is appended to every LinkedIn message and follow-up.

<Warning>
  Do not put a closing such as “Best regards” in the email signature. The configured sign-off is added separately, which would otherwise produce two closings.
</Warning>

Keep cold-email signatures lean. Use your name, role, company, and essential contact information. Avoid images and unnecessary links because they can hurt deliverability.

## Decide how much to review

Under **Settings → Schedule → Outreach delivery**, choose one of four modes:

| Mode | LinkedIn | Email |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approve LinkedIn messages | Review in the dashboard | Send automatically |
| Email drafts | Send automatically | Create drafts for you to send |
| Review both | Review in the dashboard | Create drafts for you to send |
| Send automatically | Send automatically | Send automatically |

For a new playbook or a major change, review both channels until the output is consistently correct.

<Note>
  AI-generated messages can contain errors. You remain responsible for the content sent from your accounts, even when automatic delivery is enabled.
</Note>
