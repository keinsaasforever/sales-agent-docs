Your ideal customer profile (ICP) controls contact search, candidate scoring, and eligibility for the outreach pipeline. Configure it under **Settings → Targeting**.

## Start with the free-text ICP

Describe the combination you actually want, not a broad market category. Include details such as:

- business model and industry
- company size or growth stage
- geography
- buyer role and seniority
- relevant technologies or commercial signals
- exclusions and poor-fit patterns

For example:

> DACH-based food technology companies with fewer than 50 employees and strong recent growth. Target a newly appointed Head of Marketing or Marketing Director located in DACH.

The agent evaluates this description together with the structured criteria below.

## Configure the structured criteria

| Setting | How it is used |
| --- | --- |
| **Job titles — Tier 1** | Primary roles you want to reach. Use specific decision-maker or champion titles. |
| **Job titles — Tier 2** | Executive fallback roles, such as founder or managing director, when Tier 1 is unavailable. |
| **Excluded job titles** | Roles that must be skipped even if they appear in source results. |
| **Locations** | Countries or regions to target. Leave empty for no location filter. |
| **Industries** | Industries to prioritize. Leave empty for no industry filter. |
| **Company size** | Target employee ranges. Unknown company sizes are not automatically rejected. |
| **ICP keywords** | Specialties, business models, or other terms that indicate fit. |
| **Max contacts per company** | Hard cap on people searched at each company. Three to four is the normal starting point; ten is the maximum. |

## Understand the score

Each company and contact can receive a 0–100 ICP score. The score weights are relative:

- job fit
- industry fit
- location fit

The weights do not need to add up to 100. The dashboard normalizes them. A weight of `6, 3, 1` means job fit contributes 60%, industry 30%, and location 10%.

## Use the two thresholds

### Pipeline ICP threshold

Only contacts at or above this score enter automated outreach. Set it to `0` to allow every contact.

### Phone enrichment threshold

Only contacts at or above this score receive automatic phone lookup. Below the threshold, the dashboard asks for confirmation before a manual lookup. Set it to `0` to disable the threshold.

<Warning>
  Do not lower a threshold only to fill a queue. First confirm that the source, owner assignment, exclusions, and score criteria are correct. A fuller queue with weaker candidates spends capacity and credits without improving fit.
</Warning>

## Refresh existing scores

Changing criteria can make existing scores outdated:

- If you change only the weights, use **Recompute**. The existing score inputs are recalculated without AI or credits.
- If you change the ICP meaning, use **AI rescore** so existing contacts are evaluated against the new criteria.

Newly added contacts use the current settings automatically.

## Credits and filtering

Search work can return candidates that later fail your job-title exclusions, ICP requirements, deduplication, or company checks. Credits pay for returned search or enrichment results—not only the contacts that finally enter the pipeline.

<Tip>
  When filters are granular, expect the agent to inspect more candidates than the number ultimately accepted. Start with a small company batch and review the result quality before scaling the search.
</Tip>
