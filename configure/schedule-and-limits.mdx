description: "Configure when each seat runs, how many actions it performs, and how channels and approvals behave."

Open **Settings → Schedule** to configure each seat. A seat owns its schedule, daily amounts, and channel routing. Follow-up and delivery rules are shared across the client.

## Configure the run time

For each seat:

1. select the representative
2. turn **Schedule enabled** on or off
3. choose active days
4. set the start time
5. confirm the timezone

When scheduling is off, the seat runs only when you select **Run Now**. The dashboard currently schedules one complete run per selected day.

<Warning>
  Treat the scheduled time as an automation window. Do not use LinkedIn manually while the run is active. A working window should allow roughly two to five hours for a full run, depending on the configured volume.
</Warning>

## Set daily action limits

The limits are per seat and per day:

| Limit | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **Connection requests** | New LinkedIn invitations. The dashboard caps this setting at 20. |
| **Messages** | New personalized messages generated. |
| **Outreach** | LinkedIn messages ready to send. |
| **Emails** | Initial emails. Leave empty to follow the Outreach limit. |
| **Follow-up emails** | Due email follow-ups sent that day. |
| **Follow-up LinkedIn** | Due LinkedIn follow-ups sent that day. |

The **Next run** count is the smaller of eligible contacts and the relevant daily limit. Extra contacts remain queued.

Start with low limits and increase gradually during warm-up. A normal Sales Navigator plan is built around roughly 100 contacts per week per account. Higher SSI may allow more, but neither reach nor account safety is guaranteed.

## Choose the channel behavior

For each seat, choose:

- **Always** — LinkedIn and email both run.
- **Off** — no LinkedIn actions; email can still run.
- **Prioritize emails** — email goes first and LinkedIn follows afterward.

Optional inactive-profile rules can route dormant LinkedIn profiles to email only. You can also warm up a prospect by liking up to two recent posts before connecting.

## Configure follow-ups

The client-wide maximum is:

- up to three email follow-ups
- up to one LinkedIn follow-up

Set a channel to `0` to turn its follow-ups off. Follow-ups use working-day spacing:

- first follow-up: 4 working days after the previous message
- second email follow-up: 7 working days after the previous email follow-up
- third email follow-up: 10 working days after the previous email follow-up

A reply on either channel stops automated follow-ups across both channels.

## Save and verify

After saving, return to **Home** and check:

- **Schedule** shows Active for the intended seats
- **Daily limits** match the combined active-seat capacity
- **Approval** shows the intended delivery mode
- **Next run** reflects the expected caps

If the schedule is off, **Run Now** remains available for manual runs.
