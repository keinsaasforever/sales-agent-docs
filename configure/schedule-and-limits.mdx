Open **Settings → Schedule** to configure each seat individually. A seat owns its outreach channel, run schedule, and daily limits. Cards marked **(all reps)** hold shared settings that apply across all seats. With more than one seat, pick the seat under **Rep** at the top.

You can change your own seat's channel, schedule, and daily limits. Only admins can change the shared settings.

## Choose the outreach channel

In the **Outreach channel** card, set **LinkedIn outreach** for the seat:

- **Both together** — LinkedIn and email run in parallel, so more prospects enter the pipeline each day.
- **LinkedIn first** — the connection request goes out before the first email. Prospects you cannot reach on LinkedIn are emailed right away, and anyone still waiting after a week is emailed too.
- **LinkedIn only** — no email for this seat. Outreach and scheduled searches look up no email addresses, no emails or email follow-ups are written or sent, and only people with a LinkedIn profile are taken in. Replies to emails sent earlier still come in, and a lookup you start yourself still runs.
- **Off** — no LinkedIn actions for this seat; email outreach still runs.

The card warns you when the seat has no LinkedIn account or mailbox connected; with **LinkedIn only** it does not ask for a mailbox. Connect them under **Settings → Account**.

A seat with no connected LinkedIn account does no LinkedIn work, whatever the channel says: its runs skip the LinkedIn steps and email continues. **LinkedIn first** has no invite to wait for there, so first emails go out over the working day instead of a week later. The wait returns once LinkedIn is connected.

## Configure the run time

In the **Workflow Schedule** card, complete these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable or disable the schedule">
    Toggle **Schedule enabled** on to run automatically, or leave it off to run only on demand via **Run Now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose active days">
    Under **Days**, select the days of the week the seat should run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the start time">
    Pick the **Start time**. Each run starts up to 20 minutes after this time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the timezone">
    Verify the **Timezone** matches the representative's working location.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The dashboard schedules one run per selected day. When scheduling is off, the seat remains inactive until you trigger **Run Now** manually.

<Warning>
  Treat the scheduled window as an automation period. Do not use LinkedIn manually while a run is active: concurrent activity can cause unexpected behavior. **At a glance**, at the bottom of the **Daily Limits** card, shows when LinkedIn is usually done; the run summary mail goes out then. A single run may take at most five hours; if your limits would need longer, the card tells you to lower them.
</Warning>

## When emails go out

On a seat that runs on a schedule, the run does not send emails itself. First emails and email follow-ups go out one at a time over the working day, so your mail provider does not see a burst of emails at once:

- from about 08:00 until about 17:00 in the seat's timezone, on the days the seat runs. The window moves a little each day, and now and then it starts around 07:00 or runs until about 20:00.
- at least 5 minutes apart. Seats that share a mailbox share its times.
- never in the 10 minutes before the seat's run starts.

The run still checks replies, takes on new people, writes drafts, and does all the LinkedIn work. The emails it gets ready wait for the day's times, and each email follow-up is checked for a reply once more right before it goes out. When the run starts in the afternoon, its emails go out after it until about 17:00, and the rest the next day. Emails from a run you start with **Run Now** wait for the times too.

The daily limits still count per day. About 108 emails fit into one mailbox's day at 5 minutes apart. When the email limits of all seats on a mailbox add up to more, **At a glance** says so, and the rest wait for the next day.

A mailbox gets more send times than its seats' daily limits add up to, about one and a half times as many within the same window, so an email that is ready late still goes out that day. A time with no email waiting sends nothing. The daily limits and the 5-minute gap stay as they are, so a mailbox can reach its limit earlier in the day.

- The run summary mail goes out when the run ends and says how many emails go out over the day.
- **Activity** shows the day's sends as one line per seat, **Email send times**, for example with **Emails today: 7 of 16, spread over the day**.
- Selecting **Stop** on a seat's full run also ends the rest of that day's email times for the seat. An inactive seat, a schedule switched off, or a disconnected or paused mailbox stops them too.

A seat whose schedule is off sends its emails during the runs you start.

## Set daily limits

Limits are per seat and reset each calendar day. The **Daily Limits** card groups them by channel, and each field shows what is safe for the seat right now.

| Channel | Limit | What it controls |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | **New people per day** | Connection requests sent. The dashboard caps this at 20 per day. |
| LinkedIn | **Messages to new connections per day** | First messages once an invite is accepted. |
| LinkedIn | **Follow-ups per day** | LinkedIn follow-ups when the first message gets no answer. |
| Email | **New people per day** | First emails sent. |
| Email | **Follow-ups per day** | Email follow-ups when an email gets no answer. It cannot be 0, because the same step picks up replies and bounces. To send no email follow-ups, set **Email follow-ups** to 0 instead. |

The LinkedIn limits are hidden when LinkedIn outreach is **Off**, and the **Email** limits and email lines under **At a glance** are hidden when it is **LinkedIn only**. At most the higher of the two **New people per day** numbers enter the pipeline each day, because LinkedIn and email reach the same people. People still waiting for their first message count toward it, so a run tops the pipeline up instead of growing it. The **Next run** count shown on the Home dashboard is the smaller of your eligible contacts and the relevant daily limit. Contacts beyond the cap remain queued and are picked up in a later run.

With **LinkedIn first**, more new people by email than by invite only makes the extra ones wait a week; then they are emailed without an invite. LinkedIn messages and LinkedIn follow-ups share LinkedIn's message allowance. Follow-ups start a few working days after the first message, so a new seat sends less than these numbers in its first week. A run stops when the amounts are reached, not at a fixed time.

Start low and raise the limits over the first weeks:

- **LinkedIn** warms up from the seat's first invite: about 3 a day (15 a week) in week 1, 10 a day (50 a week) from week 2, and up to 20 a day from week 4. After the warm-up, Free and Premium accounts stay at 50 a week (about 10 a day), and Sales Navigator goes to about 100 a week. A seat that runs more than five days a week spreads the weekly number thinner. The warm-up starts over after 30 days without an invite. Enter the seat's **Social Selling Index (SSI)** from Sales Navigator to help judge how high it can go.
- **Email** follows the mailbox state you select: **New mailbox** (about 3 a day, 15 a week), **Some sending history** (about 10 a day, 50 a week), or **Mailbox warmed** (no warm-up limit). The limits are per seat, so two seats that share a mailbox send twice as much from it.

No limit rises on its own. When a seat reaches the next LinkedIn warm-up week, a to-do and the notification bell offer **Raise to** the new number in one click.

A normal Sales Navigator plan supports roughly 100 contacts per week per account as a ceiling. With an SSI of 70 or more, Sales Navigator may allow up to 200 a week over time, but neither reach nor account safety is guaranteed. keinsaas applies available safety measures; you remain responsible for following [LinkedIn hygiene rules](/safety/linkedin-hygiene).

### At a glance

At the bottom of the **Daily Limits** card, **At a glance** sums up what the seat's numbers add up to. A line turns amber or red only when it needs you:

- When LinkedIn is usually done. The run summary mail goes out then, so wait for it before you use LinkedIn yourself. On a seat without LinkedIn, the line says when the run is usually done.
- **Emails go out one at a time from 08:00 until about 17:00, at least 5 minutes apart.** See [When emails go out](/configure/schedule-and-limits#when-emails-go-out).
- How many emails a day the seat sends at most once follow-ups are running.
- How many LinkedIn messages a day it sends at most. With **InMail fallback** on a Sales Navigator seat, anyone who has not accepted the invite after the waiting period gets an InMail instead, paid from your Sales Navigator InMail credits. Otherwise messages go only to people who accepted the invite or have an open profile, so usually fewer go out.
- When the next run takes on fewer new people than your limit: how many, and why. People already in the pipeline go first. When first emails wait for your approval, the line says how many, with **Approve in Messages**. Otherwise there is nothing to do: each email or invite that goes out makes room again.
- When LinkedIn is disconnected: the run skips the LinkedIn steps and is email only until it is connected.
- When the run would take longer than a single run may take: a red warning that says what to lower.

**How daily limits work** opens this section.

The **Sent per day** card shows the most the scheduled runs can send on each day of the week, for all active seats together. Fewer usually go out, because each message needs someone ready for it. Runs started by hand come on top.

## Public holidays

The Sales Agent holds outreach to a person on their public holiday: a first email, a connection request, a LinkedIn message, and both kinds of follow-up. There is nothing to switch on.

- The holidays are those of the person's country. The country comes from their location, or from the contact's **Country** when the location names none. Where the location names a region, such as Bavaria, that region's holidays count too; otherwise the national holidays count. When no country is known, outreach goes out as usual.
- A held contact is not skipped or lost. It stays in line and goes out with the next run on a day that is not a holiday for that person. It does not use up the day's limit: the next contact in line takes the slot. A seat that writes to several countries can still send fewer than its limit on a day when one country's holiday holds many contacts.
- Replies are still read on a holiday, and old invites are still withdrawn.
- The date is the date in Berlin, whatever the person's country. The **Next run** forecast does not know about holidays, so it can still count a contact who is held that day.

## Pause for a vacation

To stop a seat while its owner is away, set **From** and **Until** in the **Vacation pause** card and save. Both days are included, in the seat's timezone. Each seat has its own pause.

- Nothing goes out from the seat in that time: no first messages, invites, or follow-ups. Replies still come in.
- **Run Now** does not start a paused seat, and the **Pipeline** row on **Home** reads **Paused until** the last day.
- **Also pause search jobs** is on by default and pauses the seat's search jobs too. Your automatic search pauses only while every active seat pauses its searches.
- The card reads **Paused from … to …** before the pause, **Paused until …** during it, and **Ended on …** after it. The seat picks up again on its own. **Remove pause** clears both dates.

## Set LinkedIn options

The **LinkedIn options (all reps)** card controls how LinkedIn behaves for every seat:

- **No profile picture and few connections** — prospects without a profile picture and with fewer connections than **Connections below** get email only. Checked before the connection request.
- **No posts or comments in the last 30 days** — checked during research, after the connection request, so it skips the LinkedIn message, not the request. It needs **Export LinkedIn posts and comments** switched on in **Settings → Targeting**.
- **Like recent posts before connecting** — likes up to two of the prospect's recent posts before the connection request.
- **InMail fallback** — sends the message as an InMail when a connection request has not been accepted after the waiting period. InMails use the seat's own Sales Navigator InMail credits, so a seat without Sales Navigator sends them only to people with an open profile, who can be messaged for free. When off, messages wait for the connection. People with an open profile are messaged straight away either way.
- **LinkedIn send delay (hours)** — how long to wait after a connection request before the first LinkedIn message.

## Configure follow-ups

In the **Follow-ups (all reps)** card, the client-wide maximum follow-up counts are:

- up to **3 email follow-ups**
- up to **1 LinkedIn follow-up**

Set either channel's follow-up count to `0` to turn it off entirely. Follow-ups are spaced by working days, not calendar days:

| Follow-up | When it sends |
|---|---|
| First follow-up (any channel) | 4 working days after the previous message |
| Second email follow-up | 7 working days after the previous email follow-up |
| Third email follow-up | 10 working days after the previous email follow-up |

A reply on either channel stops all automated follow-ups across both channels for that prospect.

## Choose delivery and company stops

**Outreach delivery (all reps)** sets whether you approve every message first. See [Messaging](/configure/messaging-playbook#decide-how-much-to-review).

**When a company says yes (all reps)** decides what stops outreach to a whole company. Marking a **Meeting**, **Offer Sent**, or **Closed Deal**, or marking a reply as positive, sets the company's stage, stops outreach to its other contacts, and adds it to your blacklist. Under **What counts as a yes**, choose **Only what you mark yourself**, or **Also a reply the assistant reads as interested** to let a positive reply start this on its own.

## Save and verify

Select **Save schedule**, then return to **Home** and confirm:

- [ ] **Schedule** shows **Active** for the seats you intended to enable
- [ ] **Daily Limits** shows the new people per day you expect on LinkedIn and email
- [ ] **Approval** shows the delivery mode you configured
- [ ] **Next run** count reflects the expected caps

If a seat's schedule shows as off, **Run Now** is still available for manual runs at any time.
