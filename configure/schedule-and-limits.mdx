Open **Settings → Schedule** to configure each seat individually. A seat owns its outreach channel, run schedule, and daily limits. Cards marked **(all reps)** hold shared settings that apply across all seats. With more than one seat, pick the seat under **Rep** at the top.

You can change your own seat's channel, schedule, and daily limits. Only admins can change the shared settings.

## Choose the outreach channel

In the **Outreach channel** card, set **LinkedIn outreach** for the seat:

- **Both together** — LinkedIn and email run in parallel, so more prospects enter the pipeline each day.
- **LinkedIn first** — the connection request goes out before the first email. Prospects you cannot reach on LinkedIn are emailed right away, and anyone still waiting after a week is emailed too.
- **Off** — no LinkedIn actions for this seat; email outreach still runs.

The card warns you when the seat has no LinkedIn account or mailbox connected. Connect them under **Settings → Account**.

A seat with no connected LinkedIn account does no LinkedIn work, whatever the channel says: its runs skip the LinkedIn steps and email continues. **LinkedIn first** has no invite to wait for there, so first emails go out on the next run instead of a week later. The wait returns once LinkedIn is connected.

## Configure the run time

In the **Workflow Schedule** card, complete these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable or disable the schedule">
    Toggle **Schedule enabled** on to run automatically, or leave it off to run only on demand via **Run Now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose active days">
    Under **Days**, select the days of the week the seat should run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the start time">
    Pick the **Start time**. Each run starts up to 20 minutes after this time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the timezone">
    Verify the **Timezone** matches the representative's working location.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The dashboard schedules one run per selected day. When scheduling is off, the seat remains inactive until you trigger **Run Now** manually.

<Warning>
  Treat the scheduled window as an automation period. Do not use LinkedIn manually while a run is active — concurrent activity can cause unexpected behavior. The **Daily Limits** card shows when a run is usually done. A single run may take at most five hours; if your limits would need longer, the card tells you to lower them.
</Warning>

## Set daily limits

Limits are per seat and reset each calendar day. The **Daily Limits** card groups them by channel, and each field shows what is safe for the seat right now.

| Channel | Limit | What it controls |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | **New people per day** | Connection requests sent. The dashboard caps this at 20 per day. |
| LinkedIn | **Messages to new connections per day** | First messages once an invite is accepted. |
| LinkedIn | **Follow-ups per day** | LinkedIn follow-ups when the first message gets no answer. |
| Email | **New people per day** | First emails sent. |
| Email | **Follow-ups per day** | Email follow-ups when an email gets no answer. It cannot be 0, because the same step picks up replies and bounces. To send no email follow-ups, set **Email follow-ups** to 0 instead. |

The LinkedIn limits are hidden when LinkedIn outreach is **Off**. At most the higher of the two **New people per day** numbers enter the pipeline each day, because LinkedIn and email reach the same people. People still waiting for their first message count toward it, so a run tops the pipeline up instead of growing it. The **Next run** count shown on the Home dashboard is the smaller of your eligible contacts and the relevant daily limit. Contacts beyond the cap remain queued and are picked up in a later run.

Start low and raise the limits over the first weeks:

- **LinkedIn** warms up from the seat's first invite: about 3 a day in week 1, 10 a day from week 2, and up to 20 a day from week 4. After the warm-up, Free and Premium accounts stay at about 10 a day. The warm-up starts over after 30 days without an invite. Enter the seat's **Social Selling Index (SSI)** from Sales Navigator to help judge how high it can go.
- **Email** follows the mailbox state you select: **New mailbox** (about 3 a day), **Some sending history** (about 10 a day), or **Mailbox warmed** (no warm-up limit).

No limit rises on its own. When a seat reaches the next LinkedIn warm-up week, a to-do and the notification bell offer **Raise to** the new number in one click.

A normal Sales Navigator plan supports roughly 100 contacts per week per account as a ceiling. Higher SSI may allow more, but neither reach nor account safety is guaranteed. keinsaas applies available safety measures; you remain responsible for following [LinkedIn hygiene rules](/safety/linkedin-hygiene).

The **Sent per day** card shows the most the scheduled runs can send on each day of the week, for all active seats together. Fewer usually go out, because each message needs someone ready for it. Runs started by hand come on top.

## Public holidays

The Sales Agent holds outreach to a person on their public holiday: a first email, a connection request, a LinkedIn message, and both kinds of follow-up. There is nothing to switch on.

- The holidays are those of the person's country. The country comes from their location, or from the contact's **Country** when the location names none. Where the location names a region, such as Bavaria, that region's holidays count too; otherwise the national holidays count. When no country is known, outreach goes out as usual.
- A held contact is not skipped or lost. It stays in line and goes out with the next run on a day that is not a holiday for that person. It does not use up the day's limit: the next contact in line takes the slot. A seat that writes to several countries can still send fewer than its limit on a day when one country's holiday holds many contacts.
- Replies are still read on a holiday, and old invites are still withdrawn.
- The date is the date in Berlin, whatever the person's country. The **Next run** forecast does not know about holidays, so it can still count a contact who is held that day.

## Set LinkedIn options

The **LinkedIn options (all reps)** card controls how LinkedIn behaves for every seat:

- **No profile picture and few connections** — prospects without a profile picture and with fewer connections than **Connections below** get email only. Checked before the connection request.
- **No posts or comments in the last 30 days** — checked during research, after the connection request, so it skips the LinkedIn message, not the request. It needs **Export LinkedIn posts and comments** switched on in **Settings → Targeting**.
- **Like recent posts before connecting** — likes up to two of the prospect's recent posts before the connection request.
- **InMail fallback** — sends the message as an InMail when a connection request has not been accepted after the waiting period. InMails use the seat's own Sales Navigator InMail credits, so a seat without Sales Navigator sends them only to people with an open profile, who can be messaged for free. When off, messages wait for the connection. People with an open profile are messaged straight away either way.
- **LinkedIn send delay (hours)** — how long to wait after a connection request before the first LinkedIn message.

## Configure follow-ups

In the **Follow-ups (all reps)** card, the client-wide maximum follow-up counts are:

- up to **3 email follow-ups**
- up to **1 LinkedIn follow-up**

Set either channel's follow-up count to `0` to turn it off entirely. Follow-ups are spaced by working days, not calendar days:

| Follow-up | When it sends |
|---|---|
| First follow-up (any channel) | 4 working days after the previous message |
| Second email follow-up | 7 working days after the previous email follow-up |
| Third email follow-up | 10 working days after the previous email follow-up |

A reply on either channel stops all automated follow-ups across both channels for that prospect.

## Choose delivery and company stops

**Outreach delivery (all reps)** sets whether you approve every message first. See [Messaging](/configure/messaging-playbook#decide-how-much-to-review).

**When a company says yes (all reps)** decides what stops outreach to a whole company. Marking a **Meeting**, **Offer Sent**, or **Closed Deal**, or marking a reply as positive, sets the company's stage, stops outreach to its other contacts, and adds it to your blacklist. Under **What counts as a yes**, choose **Only what you mark yourself**, or **Also a reply the assistant reads as interested** to let a positive reply start this on its own.

## Save and verify

Select **Save schedule**, then return to **Home** and confirm:

- [ ] **Schedule** shows **Active** for the seats you intended to enable
- [ ] **Daily Limits** shows the new people per day you expect on LinkedIn and email
- [ ] **Approval** shows the delivery mode you configured
- [ ] **Next run** count reflects the expected caps

If a seat's schedule shows as off, **Run Now** is still available for manual runs at any time.
