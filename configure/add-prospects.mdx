## Add Sales Navigator searches and lists

On the **Sales Navigator** tab, paste one or more Sales Navigator URLs. The agent accepts three source types:

- a **saved search** whose URL contains `savedSearchId`
- a **people list** whose path contains `/lists/people/`
- a **company list** whose path contains `/lists/company/`

The URL sets whether the source holds **Contacts** or **Companies**. For each URL, add an optional expected result count and segment label. The expected count helps estimate size and potential credit use — it is not a guaranteed outcome. Then pick the **Owner** seat and submit.

What happens next depends on the seat:

- **The seat's LinkedIn login has Sales Navigator:** the URLs are added to that seat's saved searches, and new people from them are imported every day once an admin switches on **Import from Sales Navigator** (see below).
- **Any other seat:** keinsaas processes the searches and adds the results to your pipeline. Track progress on the **Jobs** page.

People sources already point to specific contacts but can still require research, enrichment, scoring, and filtering before anyone enters the pipeline. Company sources identify organizations: on a seat with Sales Navigator, the daily import also finds the people (see below). For any other seat, a company source still needs a contact search to find individuals.

## Import from Sales Navigator every day

Admins manage daily imports in **Settings → Targeting**:

1. In the **Sales Navigator searches** card, paste a saved search or list URL, choose the **Seat that saved it**, add an optional name, and select **Add**. Only seats whose LinkedIn login has Sales Navigator can be picked, because only the seat that saved a search can import it.
2. In the **ICP Targeting** card, switch on **Import from Sales Navigator**; the import is off until an admin switches it on. Set **Up to** how many new people arrive per day (at most 100). Then select **Save targeting**.

Each search has its own switch and shows how many of its results have been read. LinkedIn shows only the first 2,500 results of a search, so narrow a larger search to reach the rest. Importing is free. Imported people are checked, filtered, and scored like every other contact; lookups for their contact details are charged as usual. People who list no employer, for example self-employed, are skipped: they do not become contacts and no company is created for them.

For a company list or a saved company search, the Sales Agent first looks each company up and scores its **Company Fit**. It then finds people at the companies that reach your **Pipeline ICP threshold**, with a Sales Navigator search that uses your job titles, up to **Max Contacts per Company**. The line under each search counts its companies, how many have a **Company Fit** at your threshold, and the people found. A company gets its **Company Fit** once its data is complete.

## Upload a CSV

On the **Upload CSV** tab, drop a file with a header row. Commas, semicolons, and tabs all work as separators. The file can list people or companies.

The tab shows every column of your file with a sample value and the field it fills, so you can check the mapping before you import:

1. Next to **This file lists**, choose **People** or **Companies**. The Sales Agent picks one from your columns: a file with company columns and no person column is a company list. A choice you make yourself is kept.
2. In the **Our field** column, check each suggestion. Change it from the dropdown, or choose **ignore** to leave a column out. A field you pick always wins over a suggestion.

The Sales Agent reads column names it knows, including German ones such as **Vorname**, **Nachname**, **Unternehmen**, or **Webseite**. For columns whose names it does not recognize, an AI check suggests a field from the column names and up to three sample rows. **Checking the columns…** shows while it runs, and the import button waits until it is done.

The fields you can map:

| File lists | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **People** | **First name**, **Last name**, **Full name**, **Job title**, **Headline**, **Person location**, **Country**, **Language**, **Email**, **LinkedIn profile**, **LinkedIn member id**, **Sales Navigator link**, **Company name**, **Website**, **Company LinkedIn page**, **Company location**, **Industry**, **Employees**, **Owner** |
| **Companies** | **Company name**, **Website**, **Company LinkedIn page**, **Company location**, **Country**, **Industry**, **Employees**, **Description**, **Owner** |

**Employees** takes a number such as `1,200` or a size range such as `51-200`.

### People

Every row needs a LinkedIn profile URL, or a person's name together with the company's website. Rows without a LinkedIn profile URL are imported all the same; the Sales Agent completes their LinkedIn profiles afterwards, which you can follow on **Jobs**. The tab counts the rows it cannot import; open the hint next to the count to see why. Rows that only have a name or an email can be completed for you with **Request enrichment**, at 1 credit per row.

### Companies

Every row needs a company name or a website. The import adds the companies and charges no credits. It does not look for people: people at these companies come from your automatic search, so an admin switches on **Keep my pipeline full** in **Settings → Targeting** (members ask an admin). A company without a website is added, but nobody is searched there yet, because people are found through a company's website. A company that is already in the dashboard is matched, and only its empty fields are filled.

### Owner and result

Every row must also have an owner. Provide an `owner` column with each seat's owner key, or select a seat during the import step. The seat you assign also becomes the owner of the related company record and determines which connected accounts handle outreach.

For people, the result shows how many contacts were inserted, how many were **Already there**, and how many were skipped, for example because the company is on your blacklist. For companies, it shows how many were added, were already there, are on your blocked list, or have no website. Contacts outside your targeting filters are added without a seat, so no outreach starts for them.

The CSV import itself does not consume credits. Subsequent steps — completing rows, email lookup, phone lookup, or profile enrichment — may use credits.

**Before you import, run through this checklist:**

- Remove any records you are not permitted to contact.
- Include a stable identifier — such as a LinkedIn URL or business email — wherever available.
- Keep company names and domains consistent across rows.
- Add existing customers, competitors, partners, and live deals to the [blacklist](/configure/blacklist) first.

## Search existing companies

On the **People from Companies** tab, choose **Top N by priority** to search the highest-scoring companies that have no contacts yet, or **Pick specific companies** to choose them yourself. A company already searched without results is skipped unless you pick it again.

Set **Contacts per company** (the default comes from **Max Contacts per Company**) and optionally switch on **Find emails** or **Find phones** for the best new contacts. The **Estimated cost** updates as you change the settings, and a confirmation shows your balance after the search.

The search needs at least one Tier 1 title in **Settings → Targeting**, and every company needs an owner seat. It looks for Tier 1 titles first and Tier 2 titles only where no Tier 1 contact was found, and applies your locations, filters, and the **Max contacts per company** cap. Contacts outside your filters are kept without a seat.

<Warning>
  Search results can consume credits even when a returned candidate later fails granular ideal customer profile (ICP) filters or is removed as a duplicate. Review a small sample before running a large search.
</Warning>

## Search on a schedule

Admins can switch on **Keep my pipeline full** in **Settings → Targeting** and save with **Save targeting**. It searches your next-best companies by **Company Fit** once every weekday, from the hour you pick:

- **Search** sets how many companies are searched per day.
- **Pause when** sets how many people may already be waiting before the search pauses. It resumes once the queue drops.
- **Email addresses** and **Phone numbers** look up contact details right after the search instead of when contacts enter outreach.

The block shows how many credits a run reserves and how many runs your balance covers. If the balance cannot cover even the smallest run, the switch stays off. Only companies without contacts and with an owner seat are searched, and only those whose **Company Fit** is at or above your **Pipeline ICP threshold** or not scored yet.

## How contacts reach the queue

Adding a contact does not guarantee immediate appearance in every queue. Before a contact becomes an active prospect, the agent checks all of the following:

1. **Company blacklist** — the company must not be blocked.
2. **Targeting filters** — the contact must not fall outside your excluded titles, seniority levels, locations, or company sizes.
3. **Company match** — the contact's data must not point to another employer.
4. **Owner and seat assignment** — the contact must be assigned to a seat. Contacts of a paused seat wait until the seat is switched back on.
5. **Pipeline ICP threshold** — the contact's score must meet or exceed the configured minimum.
6. **Available channel and contact data** — a reachable LinkedIn profile or valid email address must be present.
7. **Current status** — the contact must not already be active, stopped, or archived.
8. **Daily limit headroom** — new people enter only within the seat's **New people per day** limits.

On **Prospects**, the **Contacts** tab shows waiting contacts as **Queued** and the rest as **Not in pipeline**; hover the badge to see why. The **Home → Next run** cards show every contact that is ready for the upcoming run. Learn how to [read the queue](/operate/next-run-queue).
