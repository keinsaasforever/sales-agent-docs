---
title: "Pipeline stage reference"
description: "Reference for every derived stage and human outcome shown in the Sales Agent."
icon: "milestone"
---

The Sales Agent derives stages from activity and stop conditions. The highest-priority applicable fact determines the displayed stage.

## Active progress stages

| Stage | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | The prospect exists but no research or outreach action is recorded yet. |
| **Researched** | Enrichment or research is available. |
| **Invite Sent** | A LinkedIn connection request was sent. |
| **Connected** | The LinkedIn connection was accepted. |
| **Messaged** | An initial email or LinkedIn message was sent. |
| **Followed Up** | At least one follow-up was sent and the sequence is not complete. |
| **To Call** | The automated sequence is complete or digital channels are exhausted, and a phone number is available. |
| **Replied** | A reply was detected on email or LinkedIn. |

## Human sales outcomes

| Stage / outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Meeting** | A meeting is booked or the conversation has moved to a person. |
| **Offer Sent** | A commercial proposal was sent. |
| **Closed Deal** | The opportunity was won. |
| **Not Interested** | The prospect declined. |
| **Lost Deal** | A progressed opportunity was lost. |
| **Unreachable** | No configured automated channel remains usable. |

Every outcome stops automated outreach.

## Archived

**Archived** hides and stops the prospect without deleting history. The previous outcome remains attached. Unarchiving restores the record, but an existing outcome continues to stop outreach.

## Precedence rules

The display follows these principles:

1. **Archived** takes precedence over everything else.
2. A human outcome such as **Closed Deal** takes precedence over a historical reply.
3. A detected reply takes precedence over normal outreach progress.
4. An unreachable prospect with a phone number appears as **To Call** rather than **Unreachable**.
5. Otherwise the stage reflects the furthest recorded automated action.

<Note>
  Returning a prospect to **In play** clears the outcome only. It does not clear its action history, so the stage is recalculated from the remaining facts.
</Note>
