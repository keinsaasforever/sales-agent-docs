Stages in the Sales Agent are **derived from activity** — they are not moved manually like cards on a board. The highest-priority applicable fact about a prospect determines which stage is displayed at any given moment.

## Active progress stages

These six stages reflect the furthest automated action recorded for a prospect.

| Stage | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | The prospect exists in the pipeline but no research or outreach action has been recorded yet. |
| **Researched** | Enrichment or research data is available for the prospect. |
| **Invite Sent** | A LinkedIn connection request has been sent and is pending acceptance. |
| **Connected** | The LinkedIn connection request was accepted. |
| **Messaged** | An initial email or LinkedIn message has been sent. |
| **Followed Up** | At least one follow-up message has been sent. |

## Reply stages

A reply on email or LinkedIn stops automated outreach on both channels. The stage shows what the reply said:

| Stage | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Replied** | A positive or neutral reply, or one not yet read by the assistant. It waits for you to answer. |
| **Declined** | A negative reply. It is not an outcome: you can still approach the person yourself later. |
| **Not right now** | A timing objection: the prospect named a later time. |

## Outcomes

Outcomes record the real-world result of a conversation. Every outcome immediately stops all further automated outreach for that prospect.

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Meeting** | A meeting is booked or the conversation has moved to a human-to-human interaction. |
| **Offer Sent** | A commercial proposal has been sent to the prospect. |
| **Closed Deal** | The opportunity was won. |
| **Not interesting** | You decided the lead is not interesting for you. |
| **Asked to stop** | The prospect asked not to be contacted again. |
| **Wrong person** | Not who you want to reach, for example someone who left the company or holds another role. |
| **Lost Deal** | A progressed opportunity did not result in a sale. |
| **Unreachable** | No configured automated channel remains usable for this prospect. |

You set outcomes yourself. The Sales Agent sets two on its own: **Unreachable** when no channel is left, and **Asked to stop** when a reply asks it to stop.

Marking **Meeting**, **Offer Sent**, or **Closed Deal**, or marking a reply as positive, also sets the company's stage on **Companies**, stops outreach to its other contacts, and puts the company on your blocked list. Under **Settings → Schedule → When a company says yes** you decide whether a reply the assistant reads as interested does the same.

## To Call list

**To Call** is a tab on **Prospects**, not a stage: a lead can be **Messaged** and due a call at the same time. A lead with a usable phone number lands there at the moment you choose under **Settings → Targeting → Call list** (by default when all follow-ups are done). A lead marked **Unreachable** with a phone number is on the list whatever you choose. A reply or any other outcome takes the lead off it.

## Archived

Marking a prospect **Archived** hides the record from standard pipeline views and stops all outreach — without deleting the prospect's history or messages. The Sales Agent also archives prospects itself, for example when you blacklist their company; the record then shows the reason, such as **Company blacklisted**.

- The previous outcome, if any, remains attached to the record.
- Unarchiving restores the record to pipeline views, but an existing outcome continues to stop automated outreach until it is cleared.

Use **Archived** for prospects you want to set aside indefinitely without losing the conversation history.

## Precedence rules

When multiple facts apply to the same prospect, the displayed stage follows these rules in order:

1. **Archived takes precedence over everything.** An archived prospect is never shown as active, regardless of any other fact.
2. **An outcome other than Unreachable takes precedence over a reply.** If you have recorded **Closed Deal** but a reply was also detected, the outcome is displayed.
3. **A detected reply takes precedence over everything below.** A prospect who replied shows **Replied**, **Declined**, or **Not right now**, even if it was later marked **Unreachable**.
4. **Unreachable comes next.** A phone number does not change the stage; it puts the lead on the **To Call** list.
5. **Otherwise, the stage reflects the furthest recorded automated action.**

<Note>
  Returning a prospect to **In play** clears the recorded outcome only. It does **not** erase action history, so the displayed stage is immediately recalculated from the remaining facts (research, sent messages, replies, etc.).
</Note>
