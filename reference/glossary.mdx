---
title: "Glossary"
description: "Definitions of the terms used throughout the Sales Agent dashboard and documentation."
icon: "book-a"
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Active seat">
    A seat that is allowed to participate in manual and scheduled pipeline runs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Blacklist">
    A client-wide list of company names and domains that must not receive outreach. Saving a match stops existing prospects immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Contact">
    A person imported, collected, or found for your account. A contact becomes a prospect when it is being worked by the outreach pipeline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Credits">
    The unit used for search and enrichment work. Returned items can consume credits even when later filtering prevents them from entering outreach.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Email draft mode">
    A delivery mode that creates email drafts in the connected mailbox for a person to review and send.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="ICP">
    Ideal customer profile: the free-text and structured rules used to evaluate company and contact fit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="ICP score">
    A normalized 0–100 fit score based on the configured criteria and relative weights.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="In play">
    A prospect without a human outcome. The prospect can be claimed by an automated step when its other eligibility conditions are met.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Next run queue">
    The Home-page forecast of contacts ready for each visible pipeline step, capped by the active seats' daily limits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Outcome">
    A human-set result such as Meeting, Offer Sent, Closed Deal, Not Interested, Lost Deal, or Unreachable. Every outcome stops automation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pipeline threshold">
    The minimum ICP score required for a contact to enter automated outreach. A value of zero turns the threshold off.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Prospect">
    A contact with outreach state and history in the Sales Agent pipeline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Seat">
    One sales representative's working identity. A seat includes one LinkedIn account, one email account, an owner, a schedule, daily limits, voice, and sign-offs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="SSI">
    LinkedIn's Social Selling Index. A higher score can support a stronger account profile but does not guarantee action limits or account safety.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Stage">
    A label derived from facts such as research, sent messages, replies, outcomes, and archive state. It is not manually moved through a board.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
