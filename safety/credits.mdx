Credits pay for search and enrichment work performed for your account. Each seat starts with 300 credits.

## What “found” means

A found item is data returned by a search or enrichment operation—for example a candidate profile, business email, or phone number. It is not a guarantee that the item satisfies every requirement in your ICP.

After data is returned, the Sales Agent can still reject or hold it because of:

- granular job-title or location filters
- excluded titles
- company or domain blacklist matches
- ICP scoring and pipeline thresholds
- duplicate detection
- wrong-company or identity checks
- missing channel eligibility

As a result, finding 20 candidates can produce fewer than 20 prospects in the outreach pipeline.

<Warning>
  The tighter and more granular your filters, the more candidates the system may need to search to find the final matches. Budget credits for the search effort, not only for the number that reaches the pipeline.
</Warning>

## Common credit-using work

- contact search at target companies
- business email lookup
- phone lookup
- additional research or enrichment requested for a contact

Uploading a CSV itself does not consume credits. Optional enrichment started during or after import can consume credits.

The exact cost can vary by the requested data and the work needed to obtain it. The dashboard shows estimates before supported manual operations and records actual usage in the credit ledger.

## Monitor usage

Open **Credits** to see:

- current balance
- total purchased
- total used
- recent transaction and usage entries

Review the balance before a large search or enrichment batch. A low balance can prevent later stages from obtaining the data they need.

## Reduce avoidable usage

- finalize your ICP and exclusions before a large search
- add the blacklist before importing or searching
- start with a small company sample
- use a phone-enrichment threshold
- avoid repeatedly searching companies that have already been processed
- correct wrong data so the agent can use the next available source intentionally

## Purchasing and cancellation

You can buy additional credits from the **Credits** page. Credits are non-refundable once granted, and unused credits are forfeited when the subscription ends. Refer to the [Sales Agent Terms](https://keinsaas.com/de/sales-agent-terms) for the binding billing rules.
