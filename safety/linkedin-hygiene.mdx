LinkedIn account safety cannot be guaranteed. keinsaas applies pacing, warm-up, withdrawal, and session-pause measures, but you remain responsible for following these practices throughout your campaign.

<Warning>
  Do not use LinkedIn manually while the Sales Agent is active or during a scheduled automation window. Running manual and automated activity at the same time is the single highest-risk behaviour you can control.
</Warning>

## Limits per account type

Your LinkedIn account type decides how many invitations and messages a week are safe. The Sales Agent reads the type from your LinkedIn login and shows a **Sales Navigator** badge on the seat under **Settings → Account** when it finds one.

| Account type | Weekly volume after warm-up |
| --- | --- |
| **Free & Premium** | up to 50 a week |
| **Sales Navigator** | 100–200 a week depending on your SSI (up to 200 from SSI 70, not guaranteed) |

The limit applies to connection requests and to LinkedIn messages separately. keinsaas strongly recommends Sales Navigator: it allows more volume and is more stable. A seat without Sales Navigator sends no InMails, except to people with an open profile; its messages wait until the invitation is accepted.

These numbers are recommendations based on LinkedIn's current behaviour, not guarantees. LinkedIn can change its limits without notice.

## Required account practices

### Warm up gradually

Start low and raise volume over the first weeks. On **Settings → Schedule**, the **Daily Limits** card shows the warm-up week for each seat and a recommended number under each field:

- week 1: up to 3 connection requests a day
- weeks 2 and 3: up to 10 a day
- from week 4: up to 20 a day on Sales Navigator, about 10 a day on Free and Premium, within the weekly limit for your account type

These are recommendations, not enforced limits: you can save higher values, except that connection requests stay capped at 20 a day.

The warm-up counts from the seat's first real invitation. After 30 days without an invitation it starts over. No limit rises on its own: when a seat reaches the next step, Home shows **LinkedIn warm-up week** with a **Raise to** button, and you decide. For the schedule settings themselves, see [Schedule and limits](/configure/schedule-and-limits).

Plan four to eight weeks, depending on how your SSI develops, before an account runs at full volume.

### Keep your SSI and profile in shape

Enter your **Social Selling Index (SSI)** on **Settings → Schedule**. It only exists with Sales Navigator; find yours at [linkedin.com/sales/ssi](https://www.linkedin.com/sales/ssi). An SSI of 70 or more may allow more weekly volume, but it does not guarantee a limit or remove the risk of restriction.

Below 30, the dashboard shows **Your SSI is below 30 – here's how to raise it**: connect with more people from your field, comment, like and repost (or post yourself), and optimise your profile. The same guide lists profile tips for buyers (headline, banner, photo, About, experience, recommendations), which you can copy or download.

### Keep one account per device

Do not run multiple automated LinkedIn accounts from the same device. Keep each account's session, browser environment, and schedule completely separate.

### Reconnect promptly

From time to time LinkedIn asks for a new sign-in. The seat then shows **Login needed**, and Home shows **LinkedIn is paused**. Connection requests, LinkedIn messages and LinkedIn follow-ups stop until you reconnect; email keeps running.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pause manual activity">
    Stop any manual LinkedIn work before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Account settings">
    In the Sales Agent, navigate to **Settings → Account** and select the seat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconnect">
    On the **LinkedIn** card, select **Reconnect** and sign in on the guided page. Your password and two-factor codes stay with LinkedIn. Only the seat's owner can connect it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    Do not start a new run until the badge shows **Connected**. LinkedIn work resumes where it left off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Reconnect the same LinkedIn account. Replacing it with a different account closes the previous account's LinkedIn conversations; see [Seats and accounts](/getting-started/seats-and-accounts).

<Warning>
  Never share your LinkedIn password or a verification code with anyone, including support.
</Warning>

### Keep pending invitations under control

A large backlog of unanswered invitations increases restriction risk. Once a week the Sales Agent withdraws invitations that have been pending for more than 30 days, while keeping your most recent pending invitations. A withdrawn prospect is tagged **Invite withdrawn** and continues by email when an address is available.

Avoid sending bursts of invitations outside of normal working patterns.

## Safety measures in the product

Depending on your configuration, the Sales Agent can apply the following safeguards automatically:

- **Pacing** — distributes actions across daily limits and scheduled windows instead of sending them in bursts; connection requests are capped at 20 a day per seat
- **Like recent posts before connecting** — likes up to 2 of a prospect's recent posts before the connection request
- **Skip inactive profiles** — moves dormant-looking profiles to email and tags them with the reason: **No profile picture and few connections** skips the connection request once both are known, and **No posts or comments in the last 30 days** skips the LinkedIn message, not the request
- **Cross-channel routing** — moves a prospect to email when LinkedIn is unavailable or blocked
- **Reply-detected stop** — halts further automated steps on both channels once a reply is detected
- **Human outcome stop** — stops every pipeline step immediately after you record an outcome
- **Follow-up window** — sends at most one LinkedIn follow-up, 4 working days after the first message, and only for first messages from the last 60 days
- **Stale invitation withdrawal** — withdraws invitations pending for more than 30 days
- **Session pause** — pauses LinkedIn work when LinkedIn asks for a new sign-in

These measures reduce risk. They do not eliminate it.

## If LinkedIn restricts the account

1. Stop all automation and manual outreach immediately.
2. Follow LinkedIn's verification or recovery instructions exactly.
3. Contact [support@keinsaas.com](mailto:support@keinsaas.com) before reconnecting the Sales Agent or resuming any schedule.

Do not attempt to work around a restriction by creating a second account on the same device or network.

Read the binding [Sales Agent Terms](https://keinsaas.com/de/sales-agent-terms#linkedin-hygiene) for the complete account-hygiene obligations.
