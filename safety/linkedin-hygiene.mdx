LinkedIn account safety cannot be guaranteed. keinsaas applies available pacing, warm-up, withdrawal, and stop measures, while you remain responsible for using the account according to these rules.

<Warning>
  Do not use LinkedIn manually while the Sales Agent is active or during a scheduled automation window. Concurrent manual and automated activity is the highest-risk behavior you can control.
</Warning>

## Required account practices

### Warm up gradually

Plan a gradual warm-up over roughly two months. Start with low limits and increase only as the account establishes normal activity. An SSI above 70 can support higher volume in some cases, but it does not guarantee a particular limit or remove restriction risk.

### Keep one account per device

Do not operate multiple automated LinkedIn accounts from the same device. Keep each account's session and working environment separate.

### Renew the session promptly

The LinkedIn session cookie normally needs periodic renewal, often around once per month. Month one can require additional re-logins while the account settles.

When notified:

1. pause manual and automated LinkedIn work
2. sign in to the correct LinkedIn account
3. open **Settings → Account** in the Sales Agent
4. paste the fresh `li_at` session cookie using the reconnect control
5. wait for confirmation before starting another run

Never email a session cookie or paste it into a support message.

### Respect the configured schedule and limits

Treat roughly 100 Sales Navigator contacts per week per account as the normal planning ceiling. Higher SSI may allow more. LinkedIn Premium without Sales Navigator generally supports less. Actual platform behavior can change and no particular volume is guaranteed.

### Keep pending invitations under control

The Sales Agent withdraws stale pending invitations at regular intervals. This keeps the pending queue healthier and allows an eligible email path to continue when LinkedIn does not connect.

## Safety measures in the product

Depending on configuration, the Sales Agent can:

- pace actions through daily limits and scheduled windows
- warm prospects by liking recent posts before connecting
- skip profiles that appear inactive
- route a prospect to email when LinkedIn is unavailable
- stop both channels after a detected reply
- stop every pipeline step after a human outcome
- withdraw stale pending invitations
- pause work when a session needs renewal

These measures reduce risk; they do not eliminate it.

## If LinkedIn restricts the account

Stop all automation and manual outreach. Follow LinkedIn's verification or recovery instructions. Contact [support@keinsaas.com](mailto:support@keinsaas.com) before reconnecting the Sales Agent or resuming a schedule.

Read the binding [Sales Agent Terms](https://keinsaas.com/de/sales-agent-terms#linkedin-hygiene) for the complete account-hygiene obligations.
