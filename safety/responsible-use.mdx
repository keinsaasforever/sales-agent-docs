The Sales Agent performs outreach on your instructions and through accounts you provide. You remain responsible for who you target, why you may contact them, and what is sent on your behalf.

## Your responsibilities

You are responsible for:

- establishing a lawful basis for processing and contacting prospects
- complying with applicable data-protection, advertising, and anti-spam rules
- supplying accurate targeting criteria, source lists, message instructions, and business claims
- reviewing AI-generated content to the degree required for your risk level
- respecting third-party platform terms and the LinkedIn hygiene rules
- keeping login credentials and session data secure
- responding to prospect rights requests as the controller of prospect data

Do not use the Sales Agent for unlawful outreach, spam, deceptive claims, prohibited data, or infringement of third-party rights.

## Controller and processor roles

For your account, subscription, billing, and use of the service, keinsaas OÜ acts as the data controller.

For the prospect data you choose to process through the Sales Agent:

- you are the controller
- keinsaas OÜ acts as your processor under your documented instructions

A Data Processing Agreement is available from [support@keinsaas.com](mailto:support@keinsaas.com).

## Session and prospect data

The service uses the LinkedIn session cookie and browser user-agent you provide solely to operate LinkedIn actions on your behalf. Do not share the cookie by email or with unauthorised people.

Prospect records can contain business contact information, profile data, research, messages, and outreach history. Give dashboard access only to people who need it and keep seat ownership current.

## AI-generated content

AI-generated messages and scores can contain errors. They can misunderstand a role, infer the wrong company context, or produce an unsupported claim. Use approvals and email drafts when a campaign, claim, or audience needs closer supervision.

<Note>
  The Sales Agent does not guarantee a response rate, sales result, search-result count, email deliverability, or uninterrupted access to a third-party platform.
</Note>
