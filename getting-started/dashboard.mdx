Use this page to learn where the dashboard shows what your agent did, what needs you, and where to change it.

## The menu

The menu on the left holds **Home**, **Tasks**, **Companies**, **Prospects**, **Messages**, and **Activity**, then **Add to Pipeline** and the **Settings** pages. Badges show open tasks, messages waiting for approval, and runs in progress.

- Admins also see **Credits** with the current balance, and the **Targeting**, **Blacklist**, and **Prompts** settings. See [Seats, roles, and accounts](/getting-started/seats-and-accounts).
- Select the search icon or press **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K**) to search people, companies, and notes.
- Collapse the menu to icons with **⌘B** (**Ctrl+B**). The dashboard remembers your choice.
- On a phone, open the menu with the button in the top-right corner. It fills the screen; close it with the **X**.
- **Support**, **Sign out**, and the light or dark theme switch sit at the bottom.

## Home

Home opens with a summary of what your agent did and what needs you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Warnings">
    Red and amber banners appear above everything else when work has stopped or is about to: **LinkedIn is paused**, **Outlook is disconnected**, **Credits running low**, or **Enrichment is paused**. Each links to the fix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activity cards">
    Seven cards count **Signals found**, **Contacts found**, **Invited**, **Accepted**, **Messaged**, **Followed up**, and **Replied** for **Last 24 hours**, **Last 7 days**, or **Last 30 days**. People count once, even when they were reached on both channels.

    Each card shows the change against an earlier span: the previous 7 or 30 days, or for **Last 24 hours** the same 24 hours one week earlier, because outreach runs on working days. Hover over the percentage to see the earlier count. No percentage appears when the earlier count is below 5.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run pipeline and Next run">
    **Run Now** starts the pipeline for your own seats. See [Run the pipeline](/operate/run-the-pipeline). The **Next run** cards forecast the work ready for each step; see [Next run queue](/operate/next-run-queue).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settings at a glance">
    **Credits**, **Schedule**, **Daily Limits**, and **Approval** show the current setting. Select a card to open the matching setting; the **Credits** card opens **Credits** for admins only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pipeline Summary and Recent Activity">
    **Pipeline Summary** counts prospects by stage; select a stage to open that list. **Recent Activity** lists the last five runs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="To do">
    One row per worklist: messages to approve, prospects who replied and wait on you, and prospects to call. Questions from keinsaas, LinkedIn warm-up suggestions, and, for admins, ICP score updates appear here too. A row stays until the work behind it is done, not until you read it. **See all** opens **Tasks**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Home shows your own seats. An admin with several seats can switch Home to one seat, **My seats**, or **All seats**. The **Next run** cards and the settings cards always cover every seat you can see.

## Tasks

**Tasks** is the full record of what needs you and what became of it:

- **Open**: every open to-do, plus the questions keinsaas asked you.
- **In progress**: questions you answered that keinsaas is working into your agent.
- **Closed**: questions keinsaas closed, with a note on what it did. A reply reopens a question.
- **Recent runs**: searches, imports, and enrichments from the last seven days.

Type your answer under a question and select **Save**. A question about one seat shows that seat's name. Your answers help keinsaas tune who your agent reaches and how it writes.

On other pages, a **Todo list** button in the bottom-right corner shows the open count. Open it to answer a question without leaving the page.

## Notifications

The bell next to the search icon shows a red count of new items. Opening it marks them as seen. It groups:

- **Needs attention**: open questions, a paused LinkedIn session, a disconnected mailbox, or no InMail credits left.
- **Suggestions**: a LinkedIn warm-up step you can accept with one click.
- **New Replies**, **Pending Approvals**, and **Call Prospect**: one line per person, opening that prospect or the message queue.
- **Completed**: finished searches, imports, and enrichments, including ones that failed or finished with issues.

## Support

Select **Support** in the menu to search this documentation or send keinsaas a message. Pick one or more topics, describe the issue in at least 20 characters, and select **Send**. keinsaas replies to your login email address.

<Warning>
  Never put passwords or LinkedIn session data in a support message.
</Warning>
