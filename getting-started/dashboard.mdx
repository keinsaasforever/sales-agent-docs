Use this page to learn where the dashboard shows what your agent did, what needs you, and where to change it.

## The menu

The menu on the left holds **Home**, **Tasks**, **Companies**, **Prospects**, **Messages**, and **Activity**, then an **Add Contacts** group with **Add to Pipeline** and **Jobs**, and the **Settings** pages. Badges show open tasks, messages waiting for approval, and runs in progress.

- Admins also see **Credits** with the current balance, and the **Targeting**, **Signals**, **Blacklist**, and **Prompts** settings. See [Seats, roles, and accounts](/getting-started/seats-and-accounts).
- Select the search icon or press **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K**) to search people, companies, and notes.
- Collapse the menu to icons with **⌘B** (**Ctrl+B**). The dashboard remembers your choice.
- On a phone, open the menu with the button in the top-right corner. It fills the screen; close it with the **X**.
- **Support**, **Sign out**, the light or dark theme switch, and the language menu sit at the bottom. See [Language](#language).

## Home

Home opens with a summary of what your agent did and what needs you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Warnings">
    Red and amber banners appear above everything else when work has stopped or is about to: **LinkedIn is paused**, **Outlook is disconnected**, **Email sending paused**, **Credits running low**, or **Enrichment is paused**. Each links to the fix.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activity cards">
    Seven cards count **Signals found**, **Contacts found**, **Invited**, **Accepted**, **Messaged**, **Followed up**, and **Replied** for **Last 24 hours**, **Last 7 days**, or **Last 30 days**. People count once, even when they were reached on both channels.

    Each card shows the change against an earlier span: the previous 7 or 30 days, or for **Last 24 hours** the same 24 hours one week earlier, because outreach runs on working days. Hover over the percentage to see the earlier count. No percentage appears when the earlier count is below 5.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Jobs and Next run">
    One row per job shows what runs: **Pipeline** first, then **Automatic search at my companies**, **Automatic Sales Navigator import**, and **Signal search**, each once it is set up. A dot blinks green while the job runs, stays green while it is on, and is grey when it is off. A row shows the schedule in words and a countdown to the next run, such as **in 2 h 15 min**, or **Off**. While a job runs, it reads **Running**.

    - **Pipeline**: the switch **Schedule on or off** turns your seat's schedule on or off. **Run Now** (play icon) starts the pipeline for your own seats, **Stop** appears while it runs, and the arrow opens **Advanced**. See [Run the pipeline](/operate/run-the-pipeline).
    - **Automatic search at my companies** and **Automatic Sales Navigator import**: admins switch them on or off here, and members see the switch but cannot change it. See [Add prospects](/configure/add-prospects).
    - **Signal search** has no switch here. It runs once keinsaas switches it on with you, and the link **Open the job** opens it under **Add to Pipeline**.

    A row says why a job does not run, for example **Doesn't run on a demo account**, **Starts once your account is activated**, or **No active seat**. The **Next run** cards below forecast the work ready for each step; see [Next run queue](/operate/next-run-queue).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settings at a glance">
    **Credits**, **Schedule**, **Daily Limits**, and **Approval** show the current setting. Select a card to open the matching setting; the **Credits** card opens **Credits** for admins only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pipeline Summary and Recent Activity">
    **Pipeline Summary** counts prospects by stage; select a stage to open that list. **Recent Activity** lists the last five runs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="To do">
    One row per worklist: messages to approve, prospects who replied and wait on you, and prospects to call. Questions from keinsaas, LinkedIn warm-up suggestions, and, for admins, ICP score updates appear here too. A row stays until the work behind it is done, not until you read it. **See all** opens **Tasks**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Home shows your own seats. An admin with several seats can switch Home to one seat, **My seats**, or **All seats**. The **Next run** cards and the settings cards always cover every seat you can see.

## Tasks

**Tasks** is the full record of what needs you and what became of it:

- **Open**: every open to-do, plus the questions keinsaas asked you.
- **In progress**: questions you answered that keinsaas is working into your agent.
- **Closed**: questions keinsaas closed, with a note on what it did. A reply reopens a question.
- **Recent runs**: searches, imports, and enrichments from the last seven days.

Type your answer under a question and select **Save**. A question about one seat shows that seat's name. Your answers help keinsaas tune who your agent reaches and how it writes.

On other pages, a **Todo list** button in the bottom-right corner shows the open count. Open it to answer a question without leaving the page.

## Notifications

The bell next to the search icon shows a red count of new items. Opening it marks them as seen. It groups:

- **Needs attention**: open questions, a paused LinkedIn session, a disconnected mailbox, a mailbox your provider blocked from sending, or no InMail credits left.
- **Suggestions**: a LinkedIn warm-up step you can accept with one click.
- **New Replies**, **Pending Approvals**, and **Call Prospect**: one line per person, opening that prospect or the message queue.
- **Completed**: finished searches, imports, and enrichments, including ones that failed or finished with issues.

## Emails from the Sales Agent

The Sales Agent also writes to you, in English or German, and each mail links back to the dashboard. Mails about a seat go to its owner, or to your account's admins when the seat has no owner.

- **Run summary**: after a pipeline run. The subject says what went out, for example "18 messages sent today". The mail counts connection requests, emails, LinkedIn messages, and follow-ups, and a **Waiting for you** box lists your to-dos: messages to approve, prospects who replied and wait on you, prospects to call, and open questions from keinsaas. A run that sent nothing, prepared nothing, and has nothing to approve sends no mail.
- **New people found**: after a contact search or a list import that found someone. It lists up to 10 people by title and company, best ICP match first, with a button that opens exactly those people on **Contacts** (the **Added since …** filter). Credits appear only when some were used.
- **Signals found**: after a buying-signal search that found something new. It lists up to 10 companies with the type and date of the event, only for signals that count: at or above your minimum signal score, still within **Use a signal for (days)**, and of a type you picked. A button opens **Companies** filtered to **Buying signals**. It is never sent for zero.
- **Weekly digest**: once a week, sent by the first pipeline run of the week (weeks start on Monday, Berlin time). It goes to every login on your account and covers last week, Monday to Sunday, compared with the week before: highlights such as meetings and positive replies, and one row for each count that is not zero, such as replies, messages sent, connection requests, new contacts, and companies with a buying signal. A percentage change shows only when the week before had at least 5. A meeting, offer, or deal counts once per company, in the week the company first reaches that stage. Outcomes and replies you file long after they happened do not count as that week's, and people you upload in a CSV or add by hand do not count as new contacts. A **Waiting for you** line adds the recipient's own to-dos. An account whose agent does not run that week gets no digest, and neither does a week with nothing in it.
- **Email sending paused**: when your mail provider blocks a mailbox. See [Activity and troubleshooting](/operate/activity-and-troubleshooting#if-your-mail-provider-blocks-sending).

An enrichment run sends no mail. The bell shows **Enrichment finished** and **Jobs** lists the run.

## Language

The dashboard comes in English and German. Open the language menu at the bottom of the menu, or on the sign-in, onboarding, and upgrade pages, and choose **English** or **Deutsch**. Your choice is saved to your login and follows you to every device. The sign-in page starts in your browser's language.

The language changes what you read in the dashboard. It does not change the language your agent writes messages in. Some texts the system writes while it runs, such as activity summaries and signal labels, are still in English.

## Support

Select **Support** in the menu to search this documentation or send keinsaas a message. Pick one or more topics, describe the issue in at least 20 characters, and select **Send**. keinsaas replies to your login email address.

<Warning>
  Never put passwords or LinkedIn session data in a support message.
</Warning>
