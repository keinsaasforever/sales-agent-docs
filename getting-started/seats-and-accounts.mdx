A seat is one sales representative: one LinkedIn account and one mailbox, with its own schedule, limits, sign-offs, and writing style. Each login on your account is an **Admin** or a **Member**, and a seat can have one owner.

## Admins and members

| | Member | Admin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Home**, **Companies**, **Prospects**, **Messages**, **Activity** | Own seats only | Every seat |
| **Run Now** | Starts own seats | Starts own seats |
| **Stop** | Stops own seats | Stops any run on the account |
| **Settings → Schedule** | Own seat's channel, schedule, and daily limits | Also the sections marked **(all reps)** |
| **Credits**, **Settings → Targeting**, **Blacklist**, **Prompts** | Not shown | Full access |
| **Seats** and **Billing** on **Settings → Account** | Not shown | Full access |

When another seat already works a company, a member sees only **A colleague is already in touch with this company.**, not who. Your role is shown under **Profile** on **Settings → Account**.

## Assign seat owners

Admins see a **Seats** card on **Settings → Account**. Pick the owner of each seat, or **Unclaimed**. Someone needs a login on your account before you can give them a seat.

The owner manages the seat's LinkedIn connection, mailbox, and sign-offs. Nobody else can, an admin included. An admin can set up a seat that has no owner yet.

To add a seat, select **Create a new seat**. This opens the support dialog with a drafted request; keinsaas sets the seat up.

## Settings → Account

The top of the page holds your **Profile**, your **Organization**, **Billing** for admins (select **Manage billing** for invoices, the payment method, or cancelling a monthly plan), and **Password**.

Below, everything belongs to one seat. If you see several seats, pick one first; the cards below then show that seat:

- **Identity**: the seat name, the LinkedIn account, and the mailbox the seat sends from. Select the pencil to rename the seat. Messages sign off with the seat name unless you set a sign-off. A warning appears when the seat sends from an account that belongs to someone else.
- **Email connection** and **LinkedIn**: see the next sections.
- **How messages end**: the sign-off for each language and the email signature.
- **Writing style**: how the agent writes for this seat. It starts from the examples given during setup; edit them to change tone, greetings, or length.

## Connect a mailbox

Under **Email connection**, select **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Outlook** and sign in with the mailbox that sends. Emails go out from that mailbox's own address. A seat uses one provider at a time: select **Disconnect** before you switch.

- If your company blocks third-party access to mailboxes, open **Can't get through? A note for your IT**, copy the note that fits, and send it to your IT team.
- When an Outlook sign-in expires, the card shows **Sign-in expired** and Home shows **Outlook is disconnected**. Emails, email follow-ups, and reply checks stop until you sign in again with the sending mailbox. LinkedIn is unaffected.
- If you connect a different mailbox to a seat with email conversations in progress, the dashboard offers to stop their email follow-ups, because those threads live in the old mailbox. It asks first when it cannot tell whether the mailbox changed. Their LinkedIn path keeps running, and new conversations start from the new mailbox.
- For a mailbox without its own LinkedIn account, select **add another email account** to send a request to keinsaas.

## Connect LinkedIn

Under **LinkedIn**, select **Connect LinkedIn**. You sign in on a guided page; your password and two-factor sign-in stay with LinkedIn. The badge shows the state:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | The login works. Hover to see when it was last checked. |
| **Connecting…** | The sign-in went through. Reload in a few seconds. |
| **Not connected** | No LinkedIn account on this seat yet. |
| **Login needed** | LinkedIn asked for a new sign-in. LinkedIn work on this seat is paused until you select **Reconnect**. |
| **Temporarily unavailable** | A problem on keinsaas's side. You do not need to do anything. |

A **Sales Navigator** badge appears when the account has Sales Navigator; it sets the weekly invite limit on **Settings → Schedule**. Open **Important: Limits per LinkedIn account type** for the weekly limits by account type, and read [LinkedIn hygiene](/safety/linkedin-hygiene).

When the session expires, Home shows **LinkedIn is paused** and connection requests, LinkedIn messages, and LinkedIn follow-ups stop. Emails continue. Reconnecting restarts LinkedIn where it left off.

## Replace a LinkedIn account or add a seat

A seat runs one LinkedIn account at a time. Select **Connect another account** to choose:

- **Replace the account on this seat**: the current account is removed. Prospects in a LinkedIn conversation stop on LinkedIn, because the new account cannot see the old invites and chats. They continue by email; anyone without an email address is closed. Select **Replace and connect**.
- **Keep both — add a new seat**: a new seat runs the second account alongside this one, and nothing on this seat changes. Select **Create a new seat** to send the request.

When you reconnect a seat whose previous login is gone, the dashboard first asks whether it is the same LinkedIn account as before. **Same account as before** carries on unchanged.
