Start with the setup wizard in the [Sales Agent dashboard](https://sales.keinsaas.com). When you finish it, the dashboard opens right away. A **Getting started** card on **Home** lists what each seat still needs before its first run, such as **Connect LinkedIn or a mailbox**, **Add your email signature**, and **Set and switch on your schedule**. Each item ticks itself once it is done. **Take the tour** shows you around, and the card's icon moves the list into **To do**. keinsaas then runs a short check and starts your outreach; everyone on your account gets an email when it is live.

Follow the steps below from configuration to a controlled first run.

## Prerequisites

You need:

- an active Sales Agent account
- at least one active seat
- a connected LinkedIn account, email account, or both for that seat (see [Seats, roles, and accounts](/getting-started/seats-and-accounts))
- the **Admin** role to change targeting, prompts, the blacklist, approvals, and other settings that apply to all seats

<Note>
  Each seat includes **300 starter credits** to fund search and enrichment. The balance shows on **Home**; admins top up under **Credits**. Check it before running large searches.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm your audience">
    Open **Settings → Targeting**. Review the ideal customer profile (ICP) description, job titles, locations, industries, exclusions, and pipeline threshold.

    Saving recalculates most existing scores at once. If you change the free-text ICP, locations, industries, or keywords, accept the **Update ICP scores?** prompt so old contacts are evaluated against the new rules. The update runs in the background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add contacts">
    Open **Add to Pipeline** and choose **Sales Navigator** for a search or list, **Upload CSV** for a list of people or companies, or **Search our database** for contacts at your existing companies. To add a few people or companies by hand, use **Quick add** on **Prospects** or **Companies**.

    Assign every contact to a seat. The owner determines which account performs the outreach.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the messaging playbook">
    Open **Settings → Prompts**. Check your company and offer, who you target, the call to action, and your success stories.

    Each seat owner then checks the seat's **Writing style** and **How messages end** (sign-offs and email signature) under **Settings → Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose delivery controls">
    Open **Settings → Schedule**. For a first run, set **Outreach delivery** to **Approve every message from the dashboard** for each seat, so every first email and LinkedIn message waits for you. Follow-ups send automatically. Keep the initial daily limits conservative.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Protect existing relationships">
    Add customers, competitors, partners, and active opportunities under **Settings → Blacklist**. Saving immediately stops matching prospects already in the pipeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the next run">
    Return to **Home**. The [**Next run**](/operate/next-run-queue) cards show how many contacts are ready for each step and how many fit under the current daily limits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the run">
    Select **Run Now** on **Home** for the complete pipeline. It starts your own seats only. Keep LinkedIn closed while the run is active. Follow progress from **Activity**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle messages and replies">
    Approve or edit queued messages under **Messages**. When a prospect replies, take over the conversation and set the appropriate outcome when it enters your sales process.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your first run is complete when **Activity** shows a terminal status and **Home → Next run** reflects the remaining queue.
</Check>

## Audience, buying signals, and your first search in the wizard

**Audience** asks **Who do you want to add to your pipeline?** The first card, **We search for you**, is the default: describe your audience and the Sales Agent finds matching companies and contacts. **Provide search URLs** takes LinkedIn or Sales Navigator saved searches and lists. **Upload a CSV** takes lists of companies, contacts, or both; in a workspace you signed up for yourself it reads **Import my own list**, and you upload the file right after setup. The columns of a file say what it holds: a file with no person column is a company list.

On an account set up with keinsaas, a file or search that brings companies only shows a short note and then only the people fields: job titles, seniority, titles to leave out, and locations, plus how many people to find per company. A file or search that brings contacts shows **Contacts provided** and folds the details away. You can still confirm them.

Where signals are on for your account, **Buying signals** is its own step after **Audience**. **Watch your companies** checks the companies on your list for news and job ads that make your offer more urgent, each company checked for 1 credit; **What you sell** tells it what to look for, **News** and **Job ads** take your own events and roles, **Suggest with AI** proposes some, and **How often we check** sets the rhythm. At least one of **News** and **Job ads** stays on. **Interest in your business** turns people who follow your company page or comment on your LinkedIn posts into leads. You can switch it on without Sales Navigator, but it reads only once a seat has a LinkedIn login with Sales Navigator, and a reminder waits in **Tasks**. **Skip** goes on without switching anything on. See [Set buying signals](/configure/icp-and-scoring#set-buying-signals).

In a workspace you signed up for yourself, the wizard always ends on **Home**. **Activate and find my first prospects** runs one first search, and only into an empty workspace where you chose **We search for you**. It waits until your audience is built into a search, about 2 minutes, and then starts on its own; **Getting started** reads **Your first search starts in a few minutes.** With your own list, Sales Navigator searches, or contacts already in, the button reads **Open my dashboard** and runs no search; **Getting started** offers **Import your list** or **Import your Sales Navigator searches** instead. Your Sales Navigator searches import once your outreach is live on a LinkedIn login with Sales Navigator. If you pick **LinkedIn without Sales Navigator** for the login, the **Review** screen warns you that the searches need Sales Navigator. Once a seat connects a login without it, admins also see a **Connect Sales Navigator** reminder under **Tasks**.
