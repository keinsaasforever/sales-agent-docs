Use this checklist after keinsaas activates your account. Before activation you complete the setup wizard in the dashboard; keinsaas then runs a short test and switches the agent on, usually within two to three business days. Open the [Sales Agent dashboard](https://sales.keinsaas.com) and follow the steps from configuration to a controlled first run.

## Prerequisites

You need:

- an active Sales Agent account
- at least one active seat
- a connected LinkedIn account, email account, or both for that seat (see [Seats, roles, and accounts](/getting-started/seats-and-accounts))
- the **Admin** role to change targeting, prompts, the blacklist, approvals, and other settings that apply to all seats

<Note>
  Each seat includes **300 starter credits** to fund search and enrichment. The balance shows on **Home**; admins top up under **Credits**. Check it before running large searches.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm your audience">
    Open **Settings → Targeting**. Review the ideal customer profile (ICP) description, job titles, locations, industries, exclusions, and pipeline threshold.

    Saving recalculates most existing scores at once. If you change the free-text ICP, locations, industries, or keywords, accept the **Update ICP scores?** prompt so old contacts are evaluated against the new rules. The update runs in the background.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add contacts">
    Open **Add to Pipeline** and choose **Sales Navigator** for a search or list, **Upload CSV** for a list of people or companies, or **People from Companies** for contacts at your existing companies.

    Assign every contact to a seat. The owner determines which account performs the outreach.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the messaging playbook">
    Open **Settings → Prompts**. Check your company and offer, who you target, the call to action, and your success stories.

    Each seat owner then checks the seat's **Writing style** and **How messages end** (sign-offs and email signature) under **Settings → Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose delivery controls">
    Open **Settings → Schedule**. For a first run, set **Outreach delivery (all reps)** to **Approve every message from the dashboard**, so every first email and LinkedIn message waits for you. Follow-ups send automatically. Keep the initial daily limits conservative.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Protect existing relationships">
    Add customers, competitors, partners, and active opportunities under **Settings → Blacklist**. Saving immediately stops matching prospects already in the pipeline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the next run">
    Return to **Home**. The [**Next run**](/operate/next-run-queue) cards show how many contacts are ready for each step and how many fit under the current daily limits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the run">
    Select **Run Now** on **Home** for the complete pipeline. It starts your own seats only. Keep LinkedIn closed while the run is active. Follow progress from **Activity**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle messages and replies">
    Approve or edit queued messages under **Messages**. When a prospect replies, take over the conversation and set the appropriate outcome when it enters your sales process.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your first run is complete when **Activity** shows a terminal status and **Home → Next run** reflects the remaining queue.
</Check>
