You do not need to monitor every internal step. Focus on exceptions, approvals, replies, and changes to your sales process.

## Before the scheduled run

- Open **Home** and work through the **To do** card: messages to approve, prospects who replied, prospects to call, and red banners such as a paused LinkedIn session or a disconnected mailbox. Admins also see when ideal customer profile (ICP) scores need updating.
- Answer open questions from keinsaas under **Tasks**.
- Review **Home → Next run** for unexpected queue sizes.
- Approve, edit, or regenerate pending email and LinkedIn messages under **Messages**, or mark the prospect **Not interesting**.
- Make sure you will not use LinkedIn manually during the scheduled working window.

## After the run

- Open **Activity** and confirm that the run succeeded or understand why it stopped.
- Review new replies first. A reply stops automated follow-ups on both channels.
- Contact prospects in **To Call** when a phone number is available. By default they join this list once the automated sequence is complete.
- Set outcomes such as **Meeting**, **Offer Sent**, or **Closed Deal** as your sales process advances.
- Check the credit balance on **Home** before starting new searches or enrichment work.

## When configuration changes

| Change | What to do next |
| --- | --- |
| ICP criteria | Save, then select **Update scores now** when prompted. The AI rescore runs in the background, and a status line shows its progress. |
| ICP weights, titles, or company sizes | Save. Existing scores are recalculated at once, with no AI. |
| Schedule or limits | Save the seat and recheck **Home → Next run**. |
| Messaging rules | Review newly generated drafts before increasing automation. |
| Blacklist | Save carefully; matching live prospects stop immediately. |
| Seat owner | The new owner checks the seat's LinkedIn and mailbox connection, sign-offs, and writing style under **Settings → Account**. |

<Tip>
  If a queue is unexpectedly empty, start with [Activity and troubleshooting](/operate/activity-and-troubleshooting) before changing limits or lowering your ICP threshold.
</Tip>
